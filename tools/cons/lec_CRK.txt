- 1986~2009: Dentin crack syndrome · Incomplete vertical root fracture · Cracked tooth syndrome(1990) · **Longitudinal tooth fracture(Rivera 1996)** · **Cracked tooth/Vertical root fracture(AAE 1997)** · Cracks/Cracked teeth(Roh 2003) · Cracked tooth syndrome(CTS; Udoye 2009).

# Mechanism(슬라이드 22~25)
- 주 표백제 = {r:Hydrogen peroxide(H2O2)} — Detailed mechanisms by which H2O2 bleaches teeth have not yet been clarified.
- H2O2 → •OH, •O, OH3 + H2O, •OH, H+ (reactive oxygen species).
- Large colored organic molecules are reduced to smaller, less noticeable molecules by oxidation.
- 슬라이드 23 흐름: High molecular weight complex organic molecule(특정 파장 반사 = Discoloration)
  - → Degradation by {r:reactive oxygen} → Lower molecular weight, less complex molecule → Reflect less light → Reduction or elimination of discoloration.
- 슬라이드 25 Chemistry of bleaching: {r:Oxidation process} — 필기: {n:반응이 진행되며 색소 분자가 더 작은 산화 생성물로 분해(그림의 끝은 H2O·CO2)}.

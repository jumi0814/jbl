# Multimorbidity(다중이환)
- The {r:coexistence of multiple chronic diseases}(= 여러 개 질환에 이환된 상태).
- Prevalence: {r:55 – 98%} in older persons.
- Major consequences: {r:disability and functional decline}, poor quality of life, and high health care cost.
# Polypharmacy(다약제복용)
- The use of multiple medications and/or the administration of {r:more medications than are clinically indicated}.
- ==Polypharmacy may cause the increased risk of inappropriate use of medications (including drug-drug interactions) and adverse effects.==
- {r:Adverse reactions} to drugs are among the leading causes of {r:hospitalization and death}.

- ② How {r:screw access holes on the facial aspect} of the restoration and prosthetic connections that are {r:deep or difficult to visualize} can be avoided (순면 쪽 나사 구멍과 깊어서 보기 힘든 연결부를 피하는 법).
- ③ How the {r:straight} multi-unit abutments {r:raise the height} of the prosthetic connections, while the {r:angled} multi-unit abutments do the same and also {r:correct for angulation}.

= Healing abutment = 수술 후 {r:잇몸을 뚫고 바깥으로 연결} · {r:직경·높이} 선택이 중요 · 요즘 {r:힐링+스캔바디} 겸용
# 역할과 선택

- ==a heterogeneous group of clinically defined conditions associated with a variable risk of progression to oral squamous cell carcinoma==.
- **potentially malignant** = the risk for cancer development is {r:increased} but {r:not inevitable}(불가피하지 않음).

- overall malignant transformation rate: {r:approximately 8%} — considerably variable by disorder type (ex. {r:PVL 44-100%}).

- **Leukoplakia(백반증)** = Leuko (white) + plakia (patch).
- Traditional definition: a white patch or plaque that cannot be characterized clinically or pathologically as {r:any other disease}; {r:a diagnosis of exclusion}(배제 진단).
- called "{r:precancerous or premalignant lesion}" in the past.
- {n:원인을 특정하기 어렵고 어느 날 갑자기 지속되는 불특정의 하얀 병소}.

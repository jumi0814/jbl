# {u:3. Cross-Linking of Fibrin by the Coagulation Cascade — 응고 cascade}
- The clotting system consists of {r:a cascade of proteolytic enzymes and cofactors}.
- Inactive precursors are activated in series, each giving rise to more of the next: "{r:Mechanism of Amplification}"(증폭).

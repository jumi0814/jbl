## Screening Tests — PT vs aPTT | 선별 검사 — 외인계 PT vs 내인계 aPTT | 10-12 | 검사 ★ · 기출 21 | jb=HM25
> tissue factor를 넣으면 PT(외인계·warfarin), partial thromboplastin을 넣으면 aPTT(내인계·heparin·혈우병)
= {r:PT} = tissue factor · extrinsic · {r:10-14 s} · warfarin↑ / {r:aPTT} = partial thromboplastin · intrinsic · {r:30-40 s} · heparin·혈우병↑
# 3. Screening laboratory tests
- a. {r:PT}(Prothrombin time) b. {r:APTT}(activated partial thromboplastin time) c. {r:TT}(thrombin time) d. {r:BT}(bleeding time (Ivy)) e. Platelet count.
- 4. Surgical procedure — {r:excessive bleeding following surgery} may be first clue to underlying bleeding problem(수술 후 과다 출혈이 첫 단서).
- Tissue Factor is used to assay prothrombin times (PT), the extrinsic coagulation pathway.
- Partial thromboplastin is used to measure the intrinsic pathway, which is called the activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT).

Mucosal incision
• Unless contraindicated, infiltrate 
the area with a local anesthetic 
containing a vasoconstrictor.
Make an incision through the 
mucosa in the vestibule. 
Between the canines the incision 
is made 10–15 mm away from 
the attached gingiva in a 
curvilinear fashion. Posterior to 
the canine the incision is only 5 
mm away from the attached 
gingiva, staying superior to the 
mental nerve.

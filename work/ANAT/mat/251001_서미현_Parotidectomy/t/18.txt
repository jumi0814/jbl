Extratemporal location of the main trunk of the FN
• FN: Exit stylomastoid foramen 1cm superior to 
mastoid process and 1cm deep to lateral surface
Common ways to identify the main trunk of the 
facial nerve 
3. Tympanomastoid (TM) suture
✓Junction of posterior bony auditory canal and mastoid portion 
of temporal bone
✓may not be visualized and can only be detected on palpation. 
✓FN trunk is usually located approximately 2-4 mm deep to the 
inferior end of the TM suture line. 
4. The styloid process
✓FN trunk is located in the inferior and superficial soft tissue.
✓The styloid process should be used with caution because it is 
situated immediately deep to the nerve.
아래 두개는 교과서적인 방법이고 임상적으로 사용하기 어려움.

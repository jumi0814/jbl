Parotidectomy - Surgical technique
(A) Separating the parotid gland from
the temporal and zygomatic branches of
the facial nerve.
(B) Freeing the parotid gland from the
facial n. branches, working from a sup.
to inf. direction.
(C) Freeing the parotid gland from the
upper facial n. branches and then from
the marginal mandibular and cervical
branches of the facial nerve.
(D) The superficial lobe of the parotid
gland separated from all the facial nerve
branches
and
still
attached
to
the
parotid (Stenson’s) duct.

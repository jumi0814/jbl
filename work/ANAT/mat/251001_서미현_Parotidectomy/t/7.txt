Medial pterygoid muscle
• Origin
• Deep head: medial side of lateral 
pterygoid plate behind the upper 
teeth
• Superficial head: pyramidal process 
of palatine bone and maxillary 
tuberosity
• Insertion
• Medial angle of the mandible
• Function
• Elevates mandible, closes jaw, helps 
lateral pterygoids in moving the jaw 
from side to side

Buccinator muscle
• Origin
• From the alveolar processes of maxilla and 
mandible, buccinator crest and 
temporomandibular joint
• Insertion
• In the fibers of the orbicularis ori
• Function
• The buccinator compresses the cheeks 
against the teeth and is used in acts such 
as blowing.
• It is an assistant muscle of mastication 
(chewing) and in neonates it is used to 
suckle.

Location of the DPA on 30 human 
skulls
• The average distance from the piriform rim to 
the DPA : 35.4 mm
• The average length of the greater palatine 
canal above the nasal floor : 10 mm
• The average distance between the 
pterygomaxillary fissure and the greater 
palatine foramen : 6.6 mm  
Li KK, Meara JG, Alexander A Jr: Location of the Descending Palatine 
Artery in Relation to the Le Fort I Osteotomy. J Oral Maxillofac Surg

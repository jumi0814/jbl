Anterior midline and posterior 
(pterygopalatine fissure)
• The subperiosteal dissection 
continues behind the 
zygomaticomaxillary
buttress into the region of 
the maxillary tuberosity and 
the pterygomaxillary fissure.
The tip of the periosteal 
elevator is always kept in 
intimate contact with the 
bony surface.

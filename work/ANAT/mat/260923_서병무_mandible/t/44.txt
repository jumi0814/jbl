Surgical flap dissection
• The incision through the mucosal 
layer in the anterior region out in the 
lip away from vestibular fold. Dissect a 
mucosal flap that retracts or is lifted 
(as shown) to expose the surface of 
the mentalis muscle. The branches of 
the mental nerve are located just 
underneath the mucosal flap and 
must be respected.
• The mentalis muscle is incised near 
the alveolar bone ridge thus creating a 
stepwise approach which protects the 
mental nerve. Later, during wound 
closure the mentalis muscle should be 
properly reattached.

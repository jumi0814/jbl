5. Identification of post. belly of 
digastric m.
• By palpating the mastoid 
tip, one can appreciate 
where the muscle enters 
the digastric groove of the 
mastoid and can search for 
the muscle by gently 
spreading through a small 
amount of the inferior 
parotid gland with the 
mosquito-curved clamp.
• Once the posterior belly of 
the digastric muscle is seen, 
the parotid gland is again 
freed, with a curved clamp, 
from the superior and 
anterior edge of this muscle 
back to the mastoid.

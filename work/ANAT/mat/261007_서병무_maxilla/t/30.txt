Subperiosteal dissections
• Sharp periosteal elevators 
are used to strip the soft 
tissues in the subperiosteal 
plane to expose the anterior 
maxillary wall, pyriform 
rims and nasal apertures, 
and zygomatico-maxillary 
buttresses.
• The periosteal dissection is 
performed in a systematic 
fashion.

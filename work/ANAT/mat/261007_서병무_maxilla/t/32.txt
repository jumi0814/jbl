Nasal cavity

elevated from the lateral 
wall and floor of the nose 
with a periosteal elevator
The anterior nasal spine and 
the lower border of the 
cartilaginous septum are 
addressed by soft-tissue 
retraction if necessary with 
a forked retractor, and the 
perichondrium on top of the 
cartilaginous septal border is 
incised

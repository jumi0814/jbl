Q: 경부 림프절 level IA와 IB의 상·하·전·후 경계를 각각 쓰시오.
A: IA(Submental) — Superior: symphysis of mandible / Inferior: body of hyoid / Anterior: anterior belly of contralateral digastric muscle / Posterior: anterior belly of ipsilateral digastric muscle. Q: Level VA·VB와 VI의 경계를 쓰시오. (VA와 VB를 나누는 높이는?)
A: VA — Superior: apex of the convergence of the sternocleidomastoid muscle and trapezius muscle / Inferior: cricoid cartilage / Anterior: posterior border of sternocleidomastoid muscle, sensory branches of cervical plexus / Posterior: anterior border of trapezius muscle. VB — Superior: lower border of cricoid cartilage / Inferior: clavicle / Anterior·Posterior는 VA와 같음. → VA/VB는 cricoid cartilage 높이(필기: omohyoid m.가 VA·VB를 나누는 경계). VI — Superior: hyoid bone / Inferior: suprasternal / Anterior·Posterior: common carotid artery. [[NECK:5]] [[NECK:23]]

Q: Clinical N staging(AJCC & UICC)에서 N1·N2a·N2b·N2c·N3의 기준을 쓰시오.
A: N1 Metastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, <3 cm in greatest dimension / N2a single ipsilateral lymph node >3 cm but <6 cm / N2b multiple ipsilateral lymph nodes, none >6 cm / N2c bilateral or contralateral lymph nodes, none >6 cm / N3 Metastasis in a lymph node >6 cm. Q: Cervical lymph node의 nodal factors affecting prognosis를 쓰시오.

                          Pulp 
• Peroxide can penetrate pulp chamber  
 
• No evidence of damage, mild inflammation in pulp        
          tissues of human teeth 
 
 • Tooth sensitivity is commonly side effect 
 
• Higher concentrations of carbamide peroxide produce  
          more sensitivity 
 
• Usually mild and transient 
  Contraindications for Bleaching 
• Large pulps  
• Hypersensitivity 
• Severe loss of enamel 
• Extensive restoration (restorations are roughened) 
• Pregnancy and nursing 
• Peroxide allergy 
       Parameters predicting prognosis 
(1) Type of Discoloration  
     - Aging, Inherent discoloration, Trauma (100%) 
     - Fluorosis (80%), Tetracycline (75%) 
 
(2) Shade of Discoloration  
       Yellow range are easier to lighten than gray or dark    
        stains such as Tetracycline or Trauma 
 
 
    Parameters predicting prognosis 
(3) Location of discoloration  
• Stains and discoloration in enamel incisal one third  
     respond very positively 
 
• Stain in dentin and gingival one third, the prognosis for 
lightening is reduced 
 
  In Office Vital Bleaching 
In-Office Bleaching (Power Bleaching)  
• Generally uses a 30% hydrogen peroxide 
whitening agent, with or without light activation

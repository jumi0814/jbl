l Dentin crack syndrome;  Brannstrom 1986
l Incomplete vertical root fracture; Wiliams 1988
l Tooth infraction; Lost 1989
l Odontiatrogenic tooth fracture; Schweizer 1989
l Cracked tooth syndrome; Ehrmann & Tyas 1990
l Longitudinal tooth fracture; Rivera 1996
l Cracked tooth/ Vertical root fracture; AAE 1997
l Cracked tooth; Zuckerman 1998
l Incomplete tooth fracture; Ellis 2001
l Cracks/Cracked teeth; Roh 2003
l Cracked tooth sysdrome(CTS); Udoye 2009

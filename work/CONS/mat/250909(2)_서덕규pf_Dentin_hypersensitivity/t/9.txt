What is Dentin Hypersensitivity?
“ Dentin Hypersensitivity is characterized by 
short, sharp pain arising from exposed 
dentin in repose to stimuli, typically 
thermal , evaporative tactile , osmotic or 
chemical and which cannot be ascribed  
to any other dental defect or pathology”
Dowell P, Addy M.  J Clin Peropdontal 1983

     Biological Properties of H2O2 
• As normal intermediate metabolite in human   
      liver -  6.48 g/day 
 
• Phagocyte cells (Neutrophils and Macrophages) 
       provide endogenous H2O2  
 
• Oxidative activities of H2O2 inactivate  
      both viruses and bacteria

     Biological Properties of H2O2 
• Oxidative reactions of free radicals with proteins, lipids, 
and nucleic acids - potential pathological consequences 
 
• Hydroxyl radicals derived from H2O2 have been implicated 
in various stages of carcinogenesis  
 
• Oxidative damage by free radicals may be associated 
with aging, stroke, and other degenerative disease

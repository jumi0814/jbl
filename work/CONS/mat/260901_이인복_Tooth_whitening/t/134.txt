In-Office Bleaching (Power Bleaching) 
• Heat & Light Activation? 
 
  1) Use of lights did not lighten teeth  
           more than bleaching gels alone 
 
  2) Light energy in the heat emitting portions have been    
            filtered out to prevent pain to non-anesthetized patients 
 
  3) Chemicals (catalyst, booster, activator) added to the  
          H2O2 gel account for the more rapid lightening 
 
134/183
- Light를 쓴다고 bleaching gel 단독보다 반드시 더 많이 하얘지는 것은 아니라는 자료를 강조했다.
- 실제 속도 증가는 gel 내 catalyst/activator의 영향일 수 있어 '빛 자체의 효과'를 과대평가하지 않는다.

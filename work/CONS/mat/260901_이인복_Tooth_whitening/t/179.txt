1) Bleaching Effects on Bonding 
• Residual peroxide and/or oxygen interferes polymerization of 
resin adhesives and restorative materials to decrease bond 
strengths.  
 
• Recommend to delay bonding procedures until 24 hours to 2 
weeks after bleaching.  
179/183
- Residual oxygen이 resin adhesive/restorative material의 polymerization을 방해하므로 bonding은 바로 하지 않는다.
- 최소 24시간은 기다리고, 상황에 따라 1-2주 정도 지연해 shade와 접착 조건이 안정된 뒤 수복하는 것이 안전하다.

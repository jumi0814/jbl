                Bleaching !! 
• Only can change color by chemical 
destruction of dye 
 
 
• Cannot improve optical characteristics 
caused by structural change

- 매우 중요: bleaching은 chemical dye를 파괴해 색을 바꾸는 치료이다.
- Fluorosis, hypoplasia 등 structural change에 의한 광학적 문제는 bleaching만으로 교정되지 않는다.

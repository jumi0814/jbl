▪ Partial demineralization
▪ Limited collagen exposure
▪ Possibility of chemical interaction
▪ Separated hydrophobic adhesive layer (2-step)
▪ Selective enamel etching required

smear layer interference 및 hydrophilic component에 의한 hydrolytic degradation 가능성을 기억한다.

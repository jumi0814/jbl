Principles of Adhesion 
▪Interaction at adhesion interface
• Physical bonding – electrostatic interaction
• Chemical bonding – ionic, covalent bonding
• Mechanical bonding – micromechanical interlocking
▪Micromechanical interlocking 
+ additional chemical bonding (functional monomer) 
- 접착 계면에는 ① electrostatic interaction 같은 약한 physical bonding, ② ionic/covalent chemical bonding, ③ micromechanical
interlocking이 함께 작용한다. 최근에는 functional monomer를 이용한 추가적 chemical bonding으로 강도와 내구성을 높이려 한다.

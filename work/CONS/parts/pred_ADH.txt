Q: Total-etch (Etch & Rinse) adhesive의 장점과 단점을 쓰시오.
A: 장점: Micromechanical interlocking / Smear layer removal / Best approach for enamel / Separated hydrophobic adhesive layer / Sufficiently thick film thickness / Stress-absorbing potential. 단점: (too) aggressive for dentin / Thick hybrid layer / Deeply exposed collagen / Vulnerable to leakage and biodegradation. Q: Self-etch adhesive의 protocol 4단계를 쓰고, 가장 중요한 동작을 쓰시오.

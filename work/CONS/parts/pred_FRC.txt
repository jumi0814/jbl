Q: Stiff metal post와 elastic FRC post의 응력 분포와 failure 양상을 비교하여 설명하시오.
A: Stiff metal post: Forces at the end of the post / Fatigue-caused failure would occur at higher stress bearing area / Unfavorable failure(root fracture). Elastic FRC post: Tooth, post, and cement all deform together / Failure appears at weakest point: adhesive joints at Core-Dentin, Post-Cement-Dentin interfaces / Favorable failure(Loss of marginal seal, Core or Post fracture, Loss of retention). Q: Favorable failure와 unfavorable failure를 구분하여 설명하시오.
A: Favorable failure = Reparable failure(complete debonding of post/core, fracture of post/core/tooth complex — 치조정 상방이라 재수복하거나 forced eruption·외과적 수술로 치아를 다시 쓸 수 있음). Unfavorable failure = Irreparable failure(crack below the bone level, root fracture — 발치해야 하는 상황). [[FRC:44]]

TEMS in Clinical Situation
•
Provides easy & efficient targeted ostectomy with a predictable direction, angulation, 
and depth via surgical guide.
•
Recommend for small (4~6 mm: trephine drill) lesion due to the risk of excessive removal 
of alveolar bone.
•
CBCT-designed surgical guide is a precise & safe method for access to the apical root  
than “freehand” approximated calculation with a periodontal probe.
•
Patients with shallow vestibule depth or excessive hyperventilation with masticatory 
muscles, and mouth opening disorder are limited for TEMS.

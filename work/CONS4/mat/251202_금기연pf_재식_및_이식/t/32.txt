Extraction Stage
1. Peritomy using #15 blade/hammer
2. Gentle use of elevator, just loosen the tooth
3. Minimal contact of forcep with tooth structures
- not damage the cementum & PDL fiber
- gently luxated and lift from socket
4. The base of socket is carefully curetted to remove
inflammatory or granulation tissues
Step 4
- 대구치를무리하게빼다보면치근이부러질수도있다. PDL를먼저끊어줘야함.
- elevator로는동요도만일으키기(최소한으로만쓰기), 그다음에포셉으로천천히
움직이며발치
- 치아에포셉이걸릴부위가없는경우, 특수한포셉쓰는방법도있음
- 치아빼면socket 내granulation tissue, cyst 긁어내기
- Atraumatic 발치하도록!
PDL cell 손상안가도록가급적CEJ 상방잡기

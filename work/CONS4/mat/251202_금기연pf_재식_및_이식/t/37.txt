Repositioning Stage
- The socket wall : gently flushed with saline 
to remove the blood clot.
- Carefully and slowly teased into original position
- Bucco-lingual cortical bone pressure for adaptation.
- The occlusion is rechecked
- Bone trimming for firm seating of tooth 
if root curvature is severe

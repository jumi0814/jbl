Post-operative Care
- Liquid diet & CHX mouth rinse for first week.
- Antibiotics are maintained for 5-7 days
- Traumatic occlusion must be prevented
- Follow-up: weekly, monthly, and semiannually.
- Most replants are firm within 4 weeks.
- Splinting can be removed at 3-4 weeks later

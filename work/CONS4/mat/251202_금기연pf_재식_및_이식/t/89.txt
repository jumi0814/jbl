1.Two-three weeks of semi-rigid splint is recommended for initial stability 
in case of need for splint. 
2. Donor tooth should have intact periodontal ligament.
3. Recipient bone should be healthy and bulky enough to accommodate a
donor tooth.
4. Surgical flap should be well approximated.
5. RCT of donor tooth should be done before transplant procedure or  
within 2 weeks after transplantation
Clinical tips for CARP transplantation

- Splint가필요하면semi-rigid로, 2-3주
- Donor tooth는PDL intact
- Recipient bone site는충분한bone 양
- Flap은잘suture하기
- 엔도는술전이나술후2-3주이내에

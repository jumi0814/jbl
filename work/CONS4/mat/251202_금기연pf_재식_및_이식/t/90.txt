Suggestions
•
Donor periodontium should be intact.
•
Recipient bone should be healthy and 
bulky enough to accommodate donor 
tooth.
•
Flap should be well approximate.
•
RCT/retro-filling should be done during 
the transplant procedure or within 2-3 
weeks.

Suggestions
•
Donor perio should be intact
•
Recipient bone should be healthy and 
bulky enough to accommodate donor 
tooth (recently extracted better)
•
Flap should be well approximated.
•
RCT should be done before the 
transplant procedure or within 2-3 
weeks after transplant.
•
Select a donor tooth without 
periodontal disease only when not 
in function!

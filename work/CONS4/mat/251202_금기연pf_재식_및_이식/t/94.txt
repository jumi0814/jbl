Why’s good for auto-transplantation?
•
Natural chewing feeling (Proprioceptor)
•
Natural gingival contour (Attached gingiva, Interdental papilla)
•
Resistance to lateral or rotating occlusal forces
•
Biological PDL attachment; shorter root acceptable, especially
when there is anatomic complications
•
Less expensive than Implant or RPD

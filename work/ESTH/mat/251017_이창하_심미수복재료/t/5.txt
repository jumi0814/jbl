Well-distribution of occlusal loads
2. Oral hygiene: poor oral hygiene
Cariostatic restorative
Restorative resistant to plaque accumulation
3. Size and nature of the caries: wide cavity
Need mechanical consideration
4. Function of the tooth: position of the tooth 
Need mechanical consideration
5. Access to the cavity: patient compliance/cooperation
Need restoratives insensitive to technique

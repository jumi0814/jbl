Tooth Preparation for DCV
▪ According to tooth position and coloration 
✓ Lingually positioned or short cervicoincisally: 
rarely require any preparation
✓ Facially positioned: some degree of preparation 
to allow space for the thickness 
✓ Discolored teeth: require preparation only if 
there is not enough space (about 1 mm) over the 
facial aspect to place a masking agent and 
composite resin

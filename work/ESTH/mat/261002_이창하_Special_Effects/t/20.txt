Techniques for Shorter Teeth
1. Shaping:
narrow gingival third mesiodistally 
→make the tooth taper and longer
flat labial middle third vertically 
→increase vertical reflecting surface
mesial and distal halves of incisal edge
→ slope toward gingiva from midline – longer
2. Staining: 
stain of higher value make the area more noticeable
white stain from body to incisal edge accentuate the height
ex) white decalcification spot at incisal edge – increase height
3. Arrangement: 
shortening laterals slightly, reopen incisal embrasure
3. 짧아보이는 치아를 길어보이게 
: 넓어보이는 치아 수정 방법과 유사!
distal쪽 줄여줄 수 있으면 좁고 길어보이게 할 수 있다

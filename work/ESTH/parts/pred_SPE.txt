Q: 상악 중절치가 상대적으로 narrower space를 가질 경우 넓어 보이게 하는 Shaping과 Staining 방법을 쓰시오.
A: Shaping — Vertical line, edge, characterization – avoid / Horizontal ones – incorporate / 1) Place the contact areas more labially and incisally: Lengthening of incisal edge · Widening of the reflective surface – make the tooth appear wider / 2) Flat and horizontal incisal edge / 3) Cuspids: move the labial ridge distally · move the contact area labially and incisally / 4) Others: move the developmental grooves more apart · curve of CEJ – flatter. Staining — Lighter body color at mesial and distal 1/3 of labial surface / Accentuate horizontal lines with light stain / Dark stain simulating cervical abrasion / Light stain, white opaque, yellow, thin orange, or brown stain – horizontally. Q: Developmental grooves를 Wider space와 Narrower space에서 각각 어떻게 옮기는가? 발육구가 보이면 치아가 어떻게 보이는지(필기)와 함께 쓰시오.

Q: Techniques for Shorter Teeth를 Shaping·Staining·Arrangement로 나누어 쓰시오.
A: 1. Shaping: narrow gingival third mesiodistally → make the tooth taper and longer / flat labial middle third vertically → increase vertical reflecting surface / mesial and distal halves of incisal edge → slope toward gingiva from midline - longer. 2. Staining: stain of higher value make the area more noticeable / white stain from body to incisal edge accentuate the height / ex) white decalcification spot at incisal edge - increase height. 3. Arrangement: shortening laterals slightly, reopen incisal embrasure. [[SPE:20]]

A: 1. Staining: dark stain or deep shade at cervical portion → decrease height. 3. Arrangement: Rotate distal aspect labially. [[SPE:21]] [[SPE:22]]

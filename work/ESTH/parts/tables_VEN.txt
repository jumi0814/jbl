#TBL Direct composite veneer (DCV) vs Porcelain laminate veneer (PLV) | k=VEN | src=[[VEN:2]] [[VEN:5]] [[VEN:6]] [[VEN:7]] [[VEN:14]]

R: 내원·비용 | {r:One visit placement} · No laboratory fees · Lower cost | 인상채득·임시치아 과정(DCV에선 생략 — 필기) · 비싸다(필기)

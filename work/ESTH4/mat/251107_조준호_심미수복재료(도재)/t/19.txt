All-Ceramic System vs Metal-Ceramic System
• All-ceramic system
• No light-blocking metal
• Wide range of translucency
• Better soft tissue health with reduced plaque 
accumulation
• Supragingival margin
• Reduced overcontouring of the emergence 
profile
• Metal-ceramic system
• Predictable structural performance
•
Single unit: porcelain cracking & bulk failure – 5-10% at 6yrs
•
Porcelain-related Structural issues – 3~4% at 10yrs
• No special knowledge required
•
routine use
Kelly JR. (2004). Dental ceramics: current thinking and trends. Dent Clin.

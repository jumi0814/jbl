• An in vitro fatigue study evaluating the fracture and fatigue resistance simulating 5-year clinical 
outcome: 3Y, 4Y, and 5Y zirconia with 0.7 and 1.2-mm thickness
• 3Y zirconia: No failure at both 0.7 mm & 1.2 mm
• 4Y zirconia: 50% fracture at 0.7 mm, but no fracture at 1.2 mm
• 5Y zirconia: 75% fracture at 0.7 mm, 35% fracture at 1.2 mm
• Tooth Preparation Guidelines
• Occlusal reduction:
• 3Y zirconia: ≥ 1 mm
• 4Y zirconia: ≥ 1.2 mm
• 5Y zirconia: ≥ 1.5 mm
• Axial wall reduction:
• ≥ 1 mm with a light chamfer margin

Limitations of All-Ceramic System
• Fracture or chipping problems
• Increased connector dimension
• Space restricted: reduced interocclusal distance
• Deep bite, supra-eruption of antagonist, short clinical crown
• Cantilever prosthesis: questionable
• Heavy bruxism or parafunctional activity

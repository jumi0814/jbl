The exported STL file of the DB crown design was imported into commercial dental CAD software (exocad DentalCAD 3.1 Rijeka),

using Anatomic Wax-up’ tool.

Asemi-adjustable digital articulator (Artex CR; Amann Girrbach) was employed in the software, and arch scan data was mounted
based on the average occlusal plane formed by the incisal edge of the maxillary central incisors and the mesiopalatal cusp tips of the
maxillary first molars. The anatomical settings of the digital articulator were adjusted to a condylar angle of 35°, a Bennett angle of

10°, and an immediate side shift of o mm.

Anterior guidance on the adjacent maxillary central incisor for protrusive movement was verified to ensure continuous contact with

the lower anterior teeth during movement.

Further modifications were made by the technician to the palatal surface of the anterior crown, focusing on achieving harmonious
anterior guidance during protrusive movement. The design of the palatal surface was assisted by using the Dynamic Occlusion’ tool

set to an intensity of o-mm to eliminate interference, specifically for protrusive movements in the digital articulator.

Anterior guidance for both designed crown and the adjacent central incisor was visually verified by continuous contact throughout

the simulated protrusion in the digital articulator, finalizing the palatal surface design. The labial surface of the data was not modified.

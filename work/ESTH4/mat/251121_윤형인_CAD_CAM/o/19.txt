Deep Learning vs. Human-expertise

* 1. Anterior crowns designed using either the DD or DA workflow achieved similar functional outcomes,

including incisal path length and deviation, compared with those designed using the CC workflow.

* 2. Both DD and DA workflows provided similar esthetic outcomes for anterior crowns, including the

width-to-height ratio, proximal contact length, and tooth axis, compared with those obtained with the
CC workflow.

Clinical Implications>

A clinician may benefit from commercially available DL-based design software solutions when designing
anterior complete crowns in clinical practice, as they can provide clinically acceptable baseline design

quality. Within the context of the current research, the DL-based workflow may augment the digital
workflow by helping users streamline the process while maintaining design quality.

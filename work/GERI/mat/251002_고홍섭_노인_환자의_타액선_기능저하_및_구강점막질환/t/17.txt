Oral Mucosa with Aging
Aging itself has no substantial effect on 
the oral mucosa or on its protective 
defense mechanisms.
Reported age-associated oral mucosal 
disorders are rather related to disease, 
malnutrition, drug usage, or wearing of 
ill-fitting dentures among elderly persons.

Oral Sensory Performance
with Aging
Taste (only a moderate effects)
- No significant reduction in taste bud 
number with age
- Reduced performance (increased 
thresholds) for salty, sour, and bitter
- No age-related decrements for sweet
- Reduction in judging taste intensity
Proc Nutr Soc 2012;71:556-565
Oral Sensory Performance
with Aging
Olfaction
- Olfactory performance declines with 
increased age.
- Older persons generally appear to be 
hyposmic.

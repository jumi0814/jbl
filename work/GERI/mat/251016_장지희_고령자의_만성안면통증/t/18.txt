Age differences in brain perception
• Decrease in EEG amplitude and increase in latency to painful 
stimuli
• Painful thermal stimuli activates: midline and central cortical 
regions (young and old), frontal and lateral sites (older adults)
 → Wider recruitment of neurons/slower cognitive processing

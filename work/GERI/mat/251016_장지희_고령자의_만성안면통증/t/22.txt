Pain in the Setting of 
Cognitive Impairment 
•
The intensity of painful conditions and the administration of 
analgesic medication seem to be inversely related as dementia 
progresses
•
Patients may have difficulty expressing the experience or inability 
to associate the actual experience due to neuropathological 
changes
•
In response to pain, cognitively impaired people might 
show more facial expressiveness

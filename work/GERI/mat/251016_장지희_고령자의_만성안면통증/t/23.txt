Pain in the Setting of 
Cognitive Impairment 
•
As dementia worsens, self-report becomes impossible and it is 
necessary to rely on pain behaviors and facial expressions
•
Abrupt changes in behavior and function might be the best 
indicators of pain
Family members and frequent caregivers can aid in 
obtaining this information.

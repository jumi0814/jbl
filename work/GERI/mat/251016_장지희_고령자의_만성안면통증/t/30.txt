Pain assessment in advanced dementia
The Pain Assessment in Advanced Dementia (PAINAD)
Assess breathing independent of vocalization
Negative vocalization
Facial expression
Body language
Consolability
each  behavior is scored 0 to 2
higher the score more severe the pain.
Scoring:
The total score range: 0-10 points 
1-3=mild pain; 
4-6=moderate pain;
7-10=severe pain.
Warden V et al. J Am Med Dir Assoc. 2003.
점수를 참고하여 통증의 정도를 유추할 수 있다.

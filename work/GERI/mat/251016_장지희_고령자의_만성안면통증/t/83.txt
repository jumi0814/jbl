Patient related barriers to effective pain 
management
Communication: Patients with communication problems with 
physician had worse pain control.
Psychological:  Anxiety, distress, depression, anger, and dementia, 
all of which can complicate assessment by masking symptoms. 
Attitudinal issues: Fear of addiction, tolerance, and side effects,  
belief that pain was inevitable.

Nonverbal Pain Indicators 
◼Changes in interpersonal interactions 
Combative, disruptive, resisting care, decreased social interactions, 
withdrawn
◼Changes in mental status 
Confusion, irritability, agitation, crying
◼Changes in usual activity 
Refusing food/appetite change, increased wandering, change in 
sleep habits

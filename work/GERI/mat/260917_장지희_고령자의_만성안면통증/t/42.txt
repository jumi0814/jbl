Symptoms of depression in elderly

▪
Disturbed sleep
▪
Self neglect
▪
Fatigue, loss of energy
▪
Irritable moods
▪
Loss of interest or pleasure in usual activities
▪
Feelings of worthlessness, excessive guilt
▪
Difficulty in concentrating
▪
Suicidal thinking

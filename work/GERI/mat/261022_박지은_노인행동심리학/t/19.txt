경도인지장애(Mild cognitive impairment, MCI)
• Cognitive concern reflecting a change in cognition 
- reported by patient or informant or clinician
• Objective evidence of Impairment in one or more cognitive domains
- Norm-adjusted interpretation: age, education, and/or cultural variation 
• Preservation of independence in functional abilities
• Not demented
Albert et al., 2011. Alz Dement
Diagnosis of MCI = “MCI syndrome”

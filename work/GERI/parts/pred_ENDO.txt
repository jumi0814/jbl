Q: Negotiation의 정의를 쓰고, negotiation에 쓰는 방법 3가지(Small K-file · C+ files · Apical abrupt precurvature)의 요점을 쓰시오.
A: Negotiation: Passive exploration(scouting) phase of canal using small files to understand canal morphology and get initial patency of the canal. Small K-file: Tactile sense w/ size 8 and 10 file, Watch-winding motion with vertical pressure. C+ files: Pyramid shaped cutting tip engages calcified canals, Stiffer than K-file. Apical abrupt precurvature: precurving 1-2mm with sharp bend(60-90°), Once successfully negotiated, perform short up-and-down movements. [[ENDO:58]] [[ENDO:59]]

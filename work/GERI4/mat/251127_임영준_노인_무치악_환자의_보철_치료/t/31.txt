Advantage of nonanatomic teeth
do not lock the mandibule in one position
permit the use of simple, less time 
consuming technique and articulators.
minimize horizontal pressures - less damage
centric relation can be used as an area rather 
than a point
adapts easily to class II & class III jaw 
relations
easier to arrange in crossbite

cusp이 없고 horizontal하기 때문에 걸리지 않아서
point가 아니라 area로!
Class II, class III, crossbite에서도 적응이 쉽다

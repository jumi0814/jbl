The World Values Survey is a global research program conducted worldwide since 1981. In its survey questionnaire, the WVS is asking about
the attitudes towards various groups, including people who have AIDS. Social distance is measured with the question “Which of these groups
you would not want to have as neighbours?’. Data is available in free access at the WVSA web-site: http://www.worldvaluessurvey org/
Chart above shows data from WVS waves 6 (2011-2014) and 7 (2017-2020).

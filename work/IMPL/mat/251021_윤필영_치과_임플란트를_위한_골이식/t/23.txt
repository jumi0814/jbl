In larger defects, 
Autogenous Bone should be considered. 
• 1-wall bony defect
: vertical or horizontal alveolar ridge augmentation
• When materials other than autogenous bone are used, 
only portions of the area in contact with the bone are 
replaced by new bone, while there is almost no new bone 
formation in the area in contact with the soft tissue. 
• If autogenous bone is not used, the healing time 
should be extended to a much longer period (over 1 
year). 
• With shorter healing periods, most of the immature 
bony tissue may break apart during the implant second 
surgery. Therefore, having a longer healing period is 
better.

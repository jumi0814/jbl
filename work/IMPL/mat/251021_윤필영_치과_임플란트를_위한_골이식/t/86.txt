Atraumatic Tooth Removal
• Avoiding tissue loss during tooth removal: Highest priority goal
• Considerations
• Brittle, Endodontically treated tooth
• A small, sharp periostome for periodontal ligament
• Interdental papillae should not be reflected
• Proper use of elevator and forceps
• Luxation force control
• Sectioning the tooth
• Soft tissue fragment or pathosis must be removed

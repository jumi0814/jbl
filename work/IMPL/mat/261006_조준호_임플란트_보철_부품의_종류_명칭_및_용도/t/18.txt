Abutment engagement
• Engaging
• Engages the implant’s rotational index
• Two-piece (separate-screw) type: applicable
• One-piece (solid screw-in) type: generally not applicable
• Provides anti-rotation and defined rotational positioning
• Non-engaging
• Does not engage the implant’s rotational index
• Two-piece (separate-screw) type: applicable
• One-piece (solid screw-in) type: applicable
• Allows rotational freedom, which may facilitate seating and accommodate minor positional or fabrication discrepancies
• In single-unit restorations, precise repositioning may be difficult without a positioning/indexing aid
• Depending on connection design, may provide a broader, more continuous contact surface by bypassing the rotational 
index

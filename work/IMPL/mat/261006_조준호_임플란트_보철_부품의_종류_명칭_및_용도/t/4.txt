임플란트 보철물 유지 방식
• 나사 유지형 (screw-retained)
• Abutment-level: restoration screw-retained to an intermediate abutment
• Conventional intermediate abutment–supported prostheses (MUA, Esthetic Abutment)
• Other abutment-level systems (HINIS, DR)
• Implant-level: restoration screw-retained directly to the implant
• UCLA-type restoration
• Ti-base–bonded restoration
• 시멘트 유지형 (cement-retained)
• Restoration cemented to a screw-retained abutment
• 나사·시멘트 유지형(screw- and cement-retained prosthesis; SCRP)
• Restoration cemented to the abutment 
• The entire restoration–abutment complex is screw-retrievable as a single unit

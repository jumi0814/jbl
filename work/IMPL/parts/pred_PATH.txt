Q: Bisphosphonate의 기능 4가지와, osteoclast에 작용하는 기전 및 그 결과를 쓰시오.
A: Inorganic pyrophosphates analogs / Affinity for hydroxyapatite crystals / Inhibitor of osteoclast activity / Bone resorption inhibition. Cancer secretes RANKL to activate osteoclasts → Osteoclasts ingest bisphosphonate and die(no resorption cavities form) → Cancer cannot invade into or through bone without osteoclastic resorption. 필기: osteoclast를 죽이므로 bone turnover가 약해진다 → osteoporosis는 improve되지만, bone remodelling이 제대로 되지 않는다.

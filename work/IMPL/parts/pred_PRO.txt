Q: Conventional concept에서 3~6개월의 치유 기간이 필요하다고 본 근거(possible biological events)를 쓰시오.
A: ① Early loading would result in fibrous encapsulation of the implant and no osseointegration ② The overheated bone tissue, which undergoes necrosis from the osteotomy preparation, needs to be replaced and during this time the tissue is not capable of supporting the implant ③ The necrotic bone created during osteotomy preparation is rapidly remodeled and turned over and during the remodeling, the strength of the bone to implant contact is compromised → it was speculated that the 3- to 6-month healing period(Szmukler-Moncler, Salama 1998). Q: 임플란트 초기 micromotion이 과도할 때 계면에서 일어나는 일과, 문헌에서 제시한 micromotion의 역치를 쓰시오.
A: If excessive micromotion is present early after implantation, the hematoma and tissue scaffold will be repeatedly damaged, thereby inducing repair by fibrous scar tissue rather than regeneration by bone at the interface(Brunski 1992). 역치: 500·150 μm 모두 fibrous membrane, threshold for bone in-growth is between 20 and 150 μm(Sobelle 1993) / 50 μm loading은 문제 없음(Vandamme 2007) / Threshold 100~150 μm(Tettamanti 2017) → Possible threshold lies somewhere between 50 and 150 μm, however this threshold should be determined according to surface state and implants design(Szmukler-Moncler 1998).

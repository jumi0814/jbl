Biomaterials- bone graft materials
• Current literatures does not provide evidence for superiority of 1 
material over another.
• Xenografts and alloplasts, may resorb at a slower rate, with their 
remnant particles existings 7 months or more after the grafting 
procedure; more-suitable for long-term alveolar ridge 
preservation.
• Allograft tends to resorb more rapidly with fewer residual 
particles and induces more newly formed bone after 3 months of 
healing; more-favorable for short-term alveolar ridge preservation.

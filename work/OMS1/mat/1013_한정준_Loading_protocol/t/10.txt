Loading timing
Disadvantages of immediate load protocol
1. Increased skill level required
2. Initial longer appointment duration: surgical/prosthetic
• Exceeding the patient’s tolerance for appointment length
• Increased possibility of medical complications
3. Possible increased implant morbidity
• Implant bone loss or implant failure, but not supported by clinical 
studies and research
• Patient’s loss of confidence in the doctor, increased costs and 
treatment time, longer treatment period

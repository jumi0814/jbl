Antley-Bixler syndrome ==

ㆍ Extremely rare craniosynostosis syndrome

* One of two gene mutation : P450 oxidoreductase gene or FGFR2
(S351C)

ㆍ Bicoronal craniosynostosis

ㆍ Rarely lambdoid and metopic
ㆍ Radiohumeral synostosis

ㆍ Midface hypoplasia

ㆍ Choanal stenosis/atresia

¢ Multiple joint contractures

ㆍ Initially lifesaving therapies

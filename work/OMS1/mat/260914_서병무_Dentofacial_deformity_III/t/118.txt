Disadvantages of CAS system
• Needs preoperative procedure 2 ~3 days
• Orthodontic surgical wire insertion  in advance 3 weeks before surgery
• Not suitable for mass production in current form
• Needs high quality 3D CT images
• Needs precise surgical procedure, less room for modification

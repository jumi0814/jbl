Rational for implant immediate-loading 
protocol
Effect of surgical trauma on healing
• The bone interface is stronger on the day of implant 
placement compared with 3 months later.
• Regional acceleratory phenomenon of bone repair around 
the implant interfaces: implant osteotomy preparation and 
implant insertion
• Around implant sites:
• Organized, mineralized lamellar bone -> unorganized, 
less mineralized, and mainly made up of woven bone.
• Weakest and most at risk for overload at 3 to 6 weeks 
after surgical insertion
• Failure in immediate implant loading: primarily between 
3 and 6 weeks implant insertion
• At 4 months: the bone is still only 60% mineralized, 
organized lamella bone.
• But proved to be sufficient in most bone types and 
clinical situations for two-stage healing and delayed 
implant loading.

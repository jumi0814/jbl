Advantages of immediate load protocol
1. Less discomfort for patients
• Decreased in discomfort and morbidity
• No second surgery -> fewer appointments
• No removable prosthesis
2. Faster treatment
3. More ideal soft tissue drape
4. Immediate satisfaction and patient acceptance
• Improvement in esthetics
• Maintenance in masticatory function and muscle mass
5. Greater bone-implant contact

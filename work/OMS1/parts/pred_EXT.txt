Q: 발치와 보존술에 쓰는 골이식재 4종의 source를 쓰고, 흡수 속도에 따라 long-term과 short-term ridge preservation에 적합한 재료를 구분하시오.
A: Autogenous — The patient / Allografts — Same species, different individual / Xenografts — Different species / Alloplasts — Synthetic. Xenografts and alloplasts, may resorb at a slower rate, with their remnant particles existings 7 months or more; more-suitable for long-term alveolar ridge preservation. Allograft tends to resorb more rapidly with fewer residual particles and induces more newly formed bone after 3 months of healing; more-favorable for short-term alveolar ridge preservation. Current literatures does not provide evidence for superiority of 1 material over another. [[EXT:23]] [[EXT:25]]

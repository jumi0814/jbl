Q: 즉시 부하(immediate occlusal loading)와 지연 하중(delayed-loading)의 개념을 각각 쓰시오.

Q: 임플란트 식립 후 과하중(overload)에 가장 취약한 시기와 그 이유를 쓰시오.
A: Weakest and most at risk for overload at 3 to 6 weeks after surgical insertion. The bone interface is stronger on the day of implant placement compared with 3 months later — around implant sites, organized, mineralized lamellar bone -> unorganized, less mineralized, and mainly made up of woven bone. Failure in immediate implant loading: primarily between 3 and 6 weeks implant insertion. [[LOAD:22]]

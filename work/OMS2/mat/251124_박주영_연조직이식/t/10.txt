조직이식의개념
Transplantation/ Graft
조직의두께에따라
Split-thickness (부분층)
A split-thickness skin graft (STSG) is a skin graft including the epidermis and part of the dermis. 
The donor site heals by re-epithelialisation from the dermis and surrounding skin and requires 
dressings.
Full-thickness (전층)
A full-thickness skin graft consists of the epidermis and the entire thickness of the dermis. The 
donor site is either sutured closed directly or covered by a split-thickness skin graft.
Composite graft (복합층)
A composite graft is a small graft containing skin and underlying cartilage or other tissue. 
Donor sites include, for example, ear skin and cartilage to reconstruct nasal alar rim defects.

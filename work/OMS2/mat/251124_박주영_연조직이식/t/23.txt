부분층피부이식(STSG)과전층피부이식(FTSG)
Advantages and disadvantages
STSG – Good for large areas of coverage, 
            Donor sites can be reharvested, 
            Fragile
            Potentially difficult to handle if meshed, 
            Prone to contracture.
FTSG – Smooth texture and pliability, 
             Thicker coverage, 
             Reduced take, 
             Poor drainage of accumulating fluid. 
             Limited choice of harvesting sites due to need primarily of the donor site.

피부이식의생착
1) 정의
이식편이수혜부에서다시혈행이재개(revascularization) 되어안착되는
것을말함
2) 생착기전
   A. 혈청침착기(0-3일diffusion)
      : fibrin anchorage and plasma circulation 
   B. 혈관문합기(2-4일)
      : revasularization by outgrowth of capillary beds
   C. 혈행재생기(4-7일)
       : definitive attachment by fibrous tissue
          re-establishment by fibrous tissue 
    D. 혈행완성기(8일이후)
       : lymphatic link-up and show nerve supply conversion of the fibrin into a 
fibrous tissue attachment

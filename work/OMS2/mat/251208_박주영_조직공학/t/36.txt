1) The first purpose of this article is that PRP increases platelet concentration when placed into grafts, 
showing the presence of at least three growth factors (platelet-derived growth factor [PDGF], transforming 
growth factor beta 1 [TGF- 1] and transforming growth factor beta 2 [TGF-2]), and indicating that 
cancellous marrow cells have receptors for these growth factors.
2) The second purpose of the article is to explore the potential of PRP to increase the rate of bone formation 
in a graft and enhance the density of the bone formed at 6 months. 
3) The third purpose of the article is to present a model of bone graft bone regeneration illustrating the 
mechanism by which PRP may enhance bone regeneration both in rate and amount.

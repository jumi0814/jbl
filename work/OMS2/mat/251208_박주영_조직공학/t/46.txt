Dr. Joseph Choukroun
Dr. Choukroun completed his degree in 
medicine in 1979, University of Montpellier, 
France. Then, in 1981 he specialized in General 
Surgery, &  Anesthesiology from, University of 
Montpellier, France. He continued his study in 
Pain Management in 1986, University of 
Strasbourg, France.
Dr. Choukroun is  the owner of Private Pain 
Clinic, Nice France. He is Inventor of the PRF 
techniques: L-PRF, A-PRF & i-PRF and is 
President of SYFAC, international symposium 
on growth factors. He is well published in some 
the most respected international Journals.

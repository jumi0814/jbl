Other immune-mediated oral mucosal 
diseases
• Systemic lupus erythematosus: antinuclear antibodies (ANAs), butterfly rash
• Systemic sclerosis: collagen deposition, microstomia
• Angioedema: IgE-mediated hypersensitivity (Type I; allergy)
• Crohn disease: granulomatous inflammation, gastrointestinal tract (ileum, colon)
• Wegener granulomatosis (Granulomatosis with polyangiitis): systemic vasculitis, 
strawberry gingivitis

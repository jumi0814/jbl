Darier's disease
• 상염색체 우성(autosomal dominant trait) 
• 상피세포간의부착기전(adherence mechanism) 이상
• erythematous pururitic papule 
• skin of trunk & scalp 
• rough texture - accum keratin 
• foul odor - bacterial deg of keratin

- 상피세포의부착기전에문제가생긴것

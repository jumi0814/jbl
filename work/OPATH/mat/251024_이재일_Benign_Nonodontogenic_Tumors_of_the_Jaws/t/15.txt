Oral Pathology
Osteoid osteoma
•
femur, tibia, phalanges  / rare in jaw
•
produce swelling of overlying soft tissue. 
•
well circumscribe radiolucency with sclerotic surroundings / 1cm 이하/ 
In or near the cortex 
•
2cm 이하이어야함/ radiopaque nidus(osteoid tissue containing 
trabeculae)가존재하기도- target-like appearance
•
surrounded by a diffuse and irregular radiolucency of vascular 
connective tissue which is surrounded by a rim of sclerotic bone.
•
Subperiosteal thickening by the formation of new bone under the 
periosteum
•
confused with that of chronic suppurative osteomyelitis. 
•
Intense pain which is out of proportion to the size of the lesion
•
nocturnal pain(PGE2) - salicyate

- 염증이동반되기도한다.
- 2cm 이하이면osteoid osteoma로주로진단하고, 이보다크면
osteoblastoma로진단한다
- osteoma는심각한nocturnal pain이특징이고,
osteoblastoma는diffuse한pian이그냥낮에나타난다.

Oral Pathology
Cementoblastoma (True cementoma)
• odontogenic lesion / identical 
feature to osteoblastoma
• Clinical & radiographic features
• mandibular molar / 25세이하/ 
slowly growing mass
• calcified mass associated with 
root / radiolucent rim
• Histopathologic features
• sheet or thick trabecular pattern 
/lined cementoblast-like cell
• Treatment & prognosis
• extraction of the involved tooth 
with the lesion

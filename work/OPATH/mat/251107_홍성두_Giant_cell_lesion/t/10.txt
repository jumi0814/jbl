2. Langhans giant cells
• Large cells found in granulomatous conditions.
• They are formed by the fusion of epithelioid cells (macrophages)
• nuclei arranged in a horseshoe-shaped pattern in the cell periphery.
• not specific for tuberculosis; found in nearly every form of 
granulomatous disease, regardless of etiology
• Granulomatous conditions
• Tuberculosis, Leprosy
• Histoplasmosis, Cryptococcosis
• Cat-scratch disease
• Sarcoidosis, Crohn's disease

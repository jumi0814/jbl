Giant cells 이란?
• a mass formed by the union of several distinct cells (usually 
macrophages), often forming a granuloma. 
• These multinucleate giant cells (MGCs) are cells of monocyte 
or macrophage lineage fused together
• It can arise in response to an infection (such as from 
tuberculosis, herpes, or HIV) or foreign body.

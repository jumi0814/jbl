Oral lymphoepithelial cyst

an uncommon lesion of the mouth that develops within oral lymphoid 
tissue
microscopically similar to the branchial cleft cyst (cervical lymphoepithelial cyst) 
but much smaller in size.

a small submucosal mass that is usually less than 1 cm in diameter
palatine tonsils, lingual tonsils, and pharyngeal adenoids
floor of the mouth, ventral surface of the tongue, and soft palate

Palatal cysts of newborn
(EPSTEIN’S PEARLS; BOHN’S NODULES)

55-80% of neonates

small, 1- to 3-mm, white or yellow-white papules

most often along the midline near the junction 
of the hard and soft palates

keratin-filled cysts that are lined by stratified 
squamous epithelium

innocuous lesions
- 신생아에서나타나며시간이지나면사라짐
- 의외로매우호발함.

[주석] - 신생아에서 나타나며 시간이 지나면 사라짐 - 의외로 매우 호발함.

Antral pseudocyst

The antral pseudocyst is a common and well-documented finding on a 
panoramic radiograph. 

Most are discovered during routine radiographic examination and have 
little clinical significance. 

The antral pseudocyst should not be confused with the mucocele of the 
sinus. 

The antral pseudocyst is believed to be caused by an inflammatory 
exudate (serum, not mucus) that accumulates under the maxillary sinus 
mucosa and results in a sessile elevation
<Antral pseudocyst>
- 상악동하방에dome shape로올라오는특징
- Mucocele과는명확하게다른특징
- 상악동점막내에염증으로인해, 염증성exudate에의해형성
- 내부에는대부분은serum에가까운exudate가관찰됨.

Metastasizing ameloblastoma
•
an ameloblastoma that has metastasized 
•
benign histopathological appearance
•
The primary tumour develops more frequently in the mandible than in the maxilla. 
•
The most common sites of metastasis are the lungs, followed by the cervical lymph nodes

Adenomatoid odontogenic tumor
• a benign encapsulated epithelial odontogenic tumour that contains rosette- or 
duct-like structures
• Localization : anterior portion of the Jaws , Mx > Mn
• Imaging : well-defined, unilocular radiolucencies extending apically past the 
cementoenamel junction
• Histology: 
• epithelial nodules
• duct-like structures
• rosette-like structures
• calcifications
• Thick capsules surrounding tumors

Oral hairy leukoplakia
• the most common EBV-related lesion in patients with
AIDS
• transplant recipients, steroid medication users, aging
individuals. 
• occurs in relatively immunocompetent individuals
• a white mucosal plaque
• does not rub off. 
• lateral border of the tongue
• faint, white vertical streaks to thickened, furrowed areas
of leukoplakia with a shaggy surface.
• Treatment
• not needed
• Systemic anti-herpesviral
• Topical treatment with retinoids or podophyllum resin
• acyclovir cream and podophyllum resin.

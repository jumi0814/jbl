금연약물로서의 작용기전
When used as an aid to smoking cessation, bupropion is thought to confer its anti-craving and anti-withdrawal 
effects by inhibiting dopamine reuptake and through the noncompetitive antagonism of the nicotinic AChR.
In addition, the stimulatory effects produced by bupropion in the CNS are similar to nicotine's effects, making low 
doses of bupropion a suitable option as a nicotine substitute.

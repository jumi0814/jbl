Anatomy of Autonomic 
Nervous System (ANS)
➢Sympathetic:
✓Preganglionic neuron cell 
bodies: located in the 
intermediolateral columns of 
spinal cord, T1 to L2/3. 
✓Sympathetic ganglions: 
• Paravertebral: 22 pairs lying on 
either side of spinal cord and 
connected to form the 
sympathetic chain, or trunk.
• Prevertebral (or collateral):      
Lie between the paravertebral 
ganglia and the target organ. 
Pelvis celiac g., inferior & 
superior mesenteric g.

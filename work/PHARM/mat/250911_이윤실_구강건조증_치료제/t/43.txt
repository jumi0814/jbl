➢Pilocarpine (Salagen tab [5mg]), Cevimeline (Evoxac cap [30mg])
• Effect depends on residual salivary gland function.
• Usually taken orally, three times a day, 30 min before each meal.
• Saliva substitute solution should be substituted for pilocarpine if 
the drug is not well tolerated, in patients with uncontrolled asthma 
(M3: Broncholar smooth muscle contration), and where there is a 
complete loss of salivary function.
General Therapeutic Use of Cholinergic Drugs: Xerostomia

Artificial Saliva Products
➢Formulations: rinses, sprays, toothpastes, gels, lozenges, and gum.
➢Mimic the chemical and physical properties of natural saliva, but do not contain 
the protective measures (ex. Immunoglobulin A) of natural saliva.
➢Main ingredients: Typically contain carboxymethylcellulose (or hydroxyethyl-
cellulose) and glycerin for viscosity, as well as calcium and phosphate ions.
➢Additional ingredients: fluoride and/or a flavoring agent/sweetener.
➢Examples of Artificial Saliva 
• Saliva-Orthana: containing naturally occurring mucin.
• Mouthkote: containing Yerba Santa (herb →식물성 당단백질).
• Moi-Stir: containing natural spearmint oil.
• Stoppers 4: containing Lysozyme and Lactoferrin.

요한 성질이고, 타액의 또 다른 기능 중 하나인 면역 기능을 대체하
기 위해 IgA와 같은 면역 성분이 들어 있는 인공 타액도 존재한다.

Sensitization
•
Central (DH of spinal cord) and 
peripheral (nociceptors) sensitization 
are the major causes of 
hypersensitivity to pain after injury.
•
allodynia, hyperalgesia
•
Sensitizing mediators: prostaglandins, 
histamine, bradykinin, protons, nerve 
growth factor
•
Central sensitization, which is a form 
of activity-dependent plasticity, occur 
when repetitive, usually high-intensity, 
synapatic transmission activates 
intracellular signal transduction 
cascades in dorsal horn neurons that 
enhance the response to subsequent 
stimuli.

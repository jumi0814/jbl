Sensitization
•
In the inflammatory pain, sensitization prevent contact with or movement of the injured 
part until repair is complete.
•
In the acute phase of central sensitization, Glutamate-activated NMDA receptor plays a 
key role
•
In the late phase of central sensitization, COX-2 begins to be expressed in neurons in 
many areas of CNS, contributing to the generalized aches, loss of appetite, and 
changes in mood and sleep cycle
•
After the nerve injury that produces hypersensitivity to pain, GABAergic interneuron 
begin to undergo apoptosis.

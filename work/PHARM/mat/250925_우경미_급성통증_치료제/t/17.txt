Classification of Pain States
Pain can be etiologically classified as neuropathic, nociceptive, nociplastic, or mixed. 
This distinction is useful, as it affects decision making at all levels of care.
Nociceptive pain 
•
results from an injury or disease affecting somatic structures such as skin, muscle, 
tendons, bone, and joints
Neuropathic pain 
•
Defined as pain caused by a lesion or disease affecting the somatosensory system
•
Common peripheral neuropathic pain states include diabetic neuropathy and radicular 
pain
•
Central neuropathic pain manifests as a constellation of symptoms following a lesion 
affecting the central nervous system. Owing to its high prevalence, the most common 
overall cause of central pain is central post-stroke pain

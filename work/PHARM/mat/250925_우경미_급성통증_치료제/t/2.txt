Pain
Definition defined by International Association for the Study of Pain (IASP) 
“an unpleasant sensory and emotional experience associated with actual or potential
tissue damage”
실질적인 혹은 잠재적인 조직손상이나, 이러한 손상에 관련하여 표현되는 감각적이고 정서적인 불유쾌한 경험
•
serves a survival function but chronic pain can be pathological
•
may occur in the absence of ongoing tissue damage
•
chronic pain may not be associated with any objective physical findings
•
always subjective; hence a patient’s report of pain should always be accepted at face 
value in the absence of evidence to the contrary
•
a complex perceptual experience affected by a multitude of factors that includes the 
activation of nociceptors (nociception), emotions, memory and cognition, social and 
cultural context, and expectations
IASP의 통증의 정의에서 주목할 점:
1. 실직적인 것 뿐 아니라, 잠재적인 것도 통증이다!
    Ex) 환자가 아프다고 왔다 -> 아플 만한 상처나 염증이 없더라도 환자가 아프다고 하면 실제로 아픈 것이다!
2.   감각적인 것 뿐 아니라 정서적인 것도 포함한다!

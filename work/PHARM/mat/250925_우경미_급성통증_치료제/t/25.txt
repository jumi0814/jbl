Drugs for Pain Management; ACETAMINOPHEN 
Acetaminophen has antipyretic and analgesic actions but no anti-inflammatory or 
antiplatelet properties.
325 to 1,000 mg q4-6h (maximum dose, 4 g/d)
It should be used at low doses in patients with liver disease (less than 2 g/d).
Adverse effects
•
The principal advantage of acetaminophen is its lack of gastric toxicity.
•
Hepatic toxicity may be serious, and acute overdose with 10 to 15 g can cause fatal 
hepatic necrosis.

Drugs for Pain Management; NSAIDs 
NSAIDs have analgesic, antipyretic, and anti-inflammatory properties mediated by 
inhibition of cyclooxygenase.
Ketorolac is an NSAID analgesic that can be given IM or IV and is often used 
postoperatively; however, parenteral therapy should not exceed 5 days due to 
nephrotoxicity.
All NSAIDs have similar efficacy and toxicities, with a side-effect profile similar to that of 
aspirin.
Adverse effects
•
NSAIDs may blunt the cardio-protective effects of aspirin.
•
NSAIDs should be used with caution in patients with impaired renal or hepatic function. 
•
The U.S. FDA has issued a boxed warning that all NSAIDs (including cyclooxygenase-
2 [COX-2] inhibitors) are associated with an increased risk of adverse cardiovascular 
thrombotic events, including MI and stroke. 
•
NSAIDs are contraindicated immediately postoperative from coronary artery bypass 
surgery.

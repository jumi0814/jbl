Drugs for Pain Management; COX-2 INHIBITORs 
COX-2 inhibitors act primarily on COX-2, an inducible form of cyclooxygenase and an 
important mediator of pain and inflammation. 
COX-2 inhibitors have little significant effect on the gastric mucosa. COX-2 inhibitors 
offer no analgesic advantage over other NSAIDs.
The currently available selective COX-2 inhibitor is celecoxib. 
Adverse effects
•
Chronic, high-dose COX-2 inhibitor increased the risk of adverse cardiovascular events 
in a study. While pharmacologic data suggest celecoxib has no effects on the platelet 
inhibitory effects of aspirin or clopidogrel, there is some concern for increased 
thrombotic risk when celecoxib is used after cardiac stenting. 
•
Cox-2 inihibitors should not be used in patients who have allergic or bronchospastic 
reactions to aspirin or other NSAIDs.
염증과 관련된 COX-2만 선택적으로 억제하면 좋겠다고 해서 만들어진 것이 selective COX-2 inhibitor이다. 대표적인 약물이 
celecoxib이다. 이 약물 계열에서 가장 중요한 부작용은 심혈관계 부작용인데, NSAID나 aspirin과 다르게 antiplatelet effect가 없기 
때문에 주의해서 사용해야 한다. 일부 약물은 해당 부작용으로 인해 시장에서 회수되기도 했음.

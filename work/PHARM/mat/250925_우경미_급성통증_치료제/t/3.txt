Pain
Pain Management
The goals should ideally include elucidating the cause of pain, mitigating nociception, 
improving function, and alleviating suffering
The treatment of pain can be roughly divided into 4 classes: 
•
Conservative (physical and psychotherapy)
•
Pharmacotherapy (opioids, non-opioid analgesics, etc)
•
Procedural interventions (injections and surgery)
•
Complementary and alternative treatments
The drugs can relieve pain by the mechanism that may interfere with:
•
the response of primary sensory neurons to somatic or visceral sensory stimuli
•
the relaying of information to the brain
•
the perceptual response to a painful stimulus
Pain management를 위한 treatment에는 다음과 같이 4가지가 
있는데 이 중 pharmacotherapy 와 procedural intervention이 
많이 사용됩니다.

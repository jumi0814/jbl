Codeine is more affected than other opioids by variations in enzyme makeup, although 
tramadol and hydrocodone are also somewhat affected by variations in activity of 
cytochrome P450 2D6.
Unlike Caucasians or Africans with a significant frequency of CYP2D6 poor 
metabolizer (PM), CYP2D6 PM is very rare in Koreans, with frequency of about 0.2% 
(Byeon et al., 2018, Arch Pharm Res).
A higher percentage of some Asian groups have slower metabolism involving 
cytochrome P450 2D6 (intermediate metabolizers) and therefore are likely to have a 
lower analgesic response to codeine than most Caucasians.

Drugs for Pain Management; OPIOID
Opioid overdose
•
Naloxone, an opioid antagonist, should be readily available for administration in the 
case of accidental or intentional overdose. 
•
Side effects include hypertension or hypotension, irritability, anxiety, restlessness, 
tremulousness, nausea, and vomiting.
•
Naloxone can also precipitate seizure activity and cardiac arrhythmias.
Antidote로는 naloxone이 사용됨을 기억하세요.

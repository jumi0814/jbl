Drugs for Pain Management; Anti-Convulsant
•
Anticonvulsants are effective for chronic, neuropathic pain by virtue of their membrane 
stabilizing properties. 
•
gabapentin, pregabalin, carbamazepine, lamotrigine, valproic acid, and clonazepam
•
effective in treating pain syndromes with an intermittent lancinating quality, such as 
trigeminal neuralgia and post-herpetic neuralgia
•
useful in syndromes characterized by continuous, burning neuropathic pain
•
Gabapentin can bind to voltage-dependent calcium channels. It is effective in diabetic 
neuropathy and trigeminal neuralgia.
•
Carbamazepine acts to block sodium channels and is used primarily to treat trigeminal 
neuralgia.
•
Clonazepam has positive effects on burning mouth syndrome (BMS)
Anticonvulsant는 membrane의 stability를 높여주는데, chronic neuropathic pain에 효과적이라고 알려져 있다. 대표적인 약물은 gabapentin, carbamazepine 등이다.
삼차신경통과 대상포진 후 신경통에 gabapentin이 쓰이고, carbamazepine 또한 삼차신경통에 사용되며,  clonazepam은 BMS에 사용된다.

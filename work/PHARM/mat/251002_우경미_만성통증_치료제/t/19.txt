Drugs for Pain Management; Anti-Depressant
Antidepressants are widely used as adjuvant therapy in pain management, particularly 
for tx. of chronic pain conditions.
antidepressants may produce analgesia; 
•
by increasing the activity of noradrenergic and serotonergic projections descending 
from the brain to the spinal cord
•
by blocking sodium channels
The analgesic effect is likely independent of their antidepressant effect, because onset 
of pain relief occurs more rapidly and at lower doses.
particularly effective in the management of post-herpetic neuralgia, diabetic 
neuropathy, migraine headache and other neuropathic pain syndromes
Tricyclic antidepressants are useful in treating pain condition, including tension-type 
headache, neck pain, migraine, and other facial pain syndromes.
In general, the least selective tricyclic agents (amitriptyline, nortriptyline, imipramine) 
are more effective than NE-selective or serotonin-selective reuptake inhibitors 
(duloxetine).
Antidepressant또한 chronic pain에 사용되는데, brain에서 spinal cord로 가는 inhibitory input을 증가시키는 기전과 sodium channel을 block 하는 기전 두가지로 진통작용을 나타낸다
고 알려져 있다. 사용용도로는 anticonvulsant와 마찬가지로 post-herpetic neuralgia, diabetic neuralgia에 쓰이기도 하고, migraine에도 사용할 수 있다.

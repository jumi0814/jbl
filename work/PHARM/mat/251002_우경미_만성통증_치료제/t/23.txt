Drugs for Pain Management; NMDA Receptor Antagonists
Because the critical role of NMDA 
receptors in the induction and 
maintenance of central sensitization, 
antagonists of the NMDA receptor are 
currently under investigation for use in 
pain treatment.
The anesthetic ketamine and the 
antitussive dextromethorphan effectively 
reduce chronic pain symptoms and 
postoperative pain.
The clinical use of these drugs are 
limited by adverse effects.
•
ketamine; psychomimetic effects
•
dextromethorphan; dizziness, fatigue, 
confusion, psychomimetic effects.

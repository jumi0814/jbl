Drugs for Pain Management; OPIOID
•
Opioids produce analgesia because of their 
action in the brain, brain stem, spinal cord, 
and peripheral terminals of primary afferent 
neurons
•
In the brain, opioids alter mood and the 
reaction to pain.
•
In the brain stem, opioids increase the activity 
of cells that provide descending inhibitory 
innervation to the spinal cord.
•
Spinal opioids inhibit synaptic vesicle release 
from primary afferents and hyperpolarize 
postsynaptic neurons.
•
There is also some evidence that peripheral 
opioid receptor activation reduces the 
activation of primary afferents.

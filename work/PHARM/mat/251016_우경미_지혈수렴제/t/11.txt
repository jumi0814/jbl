Screening Laboratory Tests 
1.  Complete Blood Count (CBC)
The CBC is normal in people with hemophilia. However, in case of heavy bleeding or bleeds 
for a long time, the hemoglobin and the red blood cell count can be low.
2.  Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT) Test (WNL: 30-40 seconds)
It measures the clotting ability of factors VIII (8), IX (9), XI (11), and XII (12). A longer clotting 
time can be shown in patients with heparin therapy; hemophilia A or B.
3.  Prothrombin Time (PT) Test (WNL: 10-14 seconds)
It measures primarily the clotting ability of factors I, II, V, VII, and X. If any of these factors 
are too low, it takes longer than normal for the blood to clot. The results of this test will be 
normal among most people with hemophilia A and B, but abnormal in the people taking 
Warfarin (Vitamin K antagonist).
4.  Fibrinogen (clotting factor I) Test
This test is ordered either along with other blood clotting tests or when a patient has an 
abnormal PT or aPTT result, or both.

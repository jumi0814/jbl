1. Mechanical Aids
· compression, hemostat, modeling compound splint bone wax or ligature 
· effective against a rapid and profuse loss of blood from a large vessel
2. Local Hemostatics
· Agents acting to arrest the flow of blood 
· Hemostatics arrest bleeding either by the formation of an artificial clot or 
by providing a mechanical matrix that facilitates clotting 
when applied directly to the bleeding surface 
· absorbable hemostatic agents, coagulation-modifying agents, topical vasoconstrictor, 
or anti-fibrinolysis agents
· effective for the control of continued, slow bleeding (oozing)
* Astringents; chemicals that shrinks or constricts body tissues to reduce bleeding from 
minor abrasions 
Bleeding Control Measures
Mechanical aid (compression) 이 지혈에 사용될 수 있고, astringent가 사용될 수 있습니다. Astringent는 
protein을 denaturation을 시켜 그 부위에 clot을 형성하는 것이기 때문에 유해한 작용들이 있어 많이 사용되
지는 않습니다.

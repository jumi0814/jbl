Absorbable Gelatin Sponge (Gelfoam)
5) Precautions 
•
should not be used in closure of skin incisions because it may interfere with the 
healing of skin edges. This is due to mechanical interposition of gelatin and is not 
secondary to intrinsic interference with wound healing.
•
should not be placed in intravascular compartments because of the risk of 
embolization.
•
do not use in patients with known allergies to porcine collagen.
•
use sterile product and cannot be re-sterilized.
•
giant-cell granuloma has been reported at the implantation site following use of 
absorbable gelatin sponge in closed space.

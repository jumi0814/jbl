Gelatin-Thrombin Matrix (GTM) Granule (FloSeal) 
1) General Features
•
a combination of the gelatin granule matrix and the thrombin: Floseal consists of a 
bovine-derived Gelatin Matrix component and a human-derived Thrombin (extracted 
from pooled human plasma) component.
•
not recommended due to concerns about its safety, cost, and availability. 
•
it may prove useful when conventional hemostatic methods (suturing and 
electrocauterization) fail or are not appropriate
•
absorbed without any residue over the course of 6-8 weeks.
2) Mode of Action
•
has both mechanical and active ingredients to achieve hemostasis.
•
first, the gelatin matrix fills the bleeding site with gelatin granules and swells, 
generating a stable clot. A spontaneously forming clot also triggers contact activation 
of platelets.
•
next, the thrombin component converts fibrinogen into a fibrin polymer

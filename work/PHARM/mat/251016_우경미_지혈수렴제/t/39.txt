Gelatin-Thrombin Matrix (GTM) Granule (FloSeal) 
3) Clinical Uses
•
it may prove useful when conventional hemostatic methods (suturing and 
electrocauterization) fail or are not appropriate
4) Precautions
•
Do not inject or compress GTM into blood vessels.
•
Do not apply GTM in the absence of active blood flow.
•
Rare but serious adverse effects and complications have been reported such as fatal 
or near-fatal thromboembolism and small bowel obstruction.

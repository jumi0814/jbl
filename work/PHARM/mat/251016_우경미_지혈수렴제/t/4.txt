Perioperative Anticoagulation and Antithrombotic Management
•
A dentist has three options: either to stop anticoagulation, to continue it, or to bridge 
with heparin (LMWH).
•
Patients of Typical Dental Procedures
No need to discontinue medication; use local measures to control bleeding
•
Patients with Higher Risk of Bleeding
* Any suggested modification to the medication regimen prior to dental surgery should be 
done in consultation with and on advice of the patient’s physician
* The serious risks of stopping or reducing the anti-thromboembolic medication regimens 
need to be balanced against the potential consequences of prolonged bleeding, which can 
be controlled with local measures

Oxidized Cellulose (Novocell, Oxycel)
1) General Features
•
formed from plant material (polyhydroglucuronic acid) that undergoes oxidization by 
nitrogen dioxide to create cellulosic acid. 
•
soluble in alkali, but insoluble in water or acid solutions.
•
absorption begins 2-7 days after implantation and completed at 6 weeks or longer.
•
removal before complete dissolution can be facilitated by irrigation with saline or mildly 
alkaline solution. 
•
antimicrobial properties against a number of pathogenic bacteria
•
creates the low pH environment and deactivates other biologic agents (e.g. thrombin), 
and may also lead to increased inflammation and delayed healing

1) General Features
•
prepared from alpha-cellulose by reaction with alkali to form viscose then spun and 
oxidized
•
less friable and has less tendency to stick to instruments and gloves than oxidized 
cellulose
•
used extensively in oral and maxillofacial surgery to control intrabony arterial bleedings 
from the inferior alveolar artery
•
do not retard epithelialization
•
other features (e.g. antimicrobial properties) are almost the same as oxidized cellulose
2) Mode of Action
•
formation of a gelatinous mass upon saturation with blood, which leads to formation of 
a stable clot

1) General Features
•
active absorbable collagen hemostat
•
used for accelerating clot formation
2) Mode of Action
•
attract platelets that adhere to the fibril and undergo release phenomenon to trigger 
the aggregation of platelets
3) Clinical Uses 
•
should be used dry.
•
used in all surgical applications
•
not for injection, intraocular or intravascular use.
4) Preparations
•
sheets, flour (preloaded syringe), sponge.
•
sheets for flat surfaces or to wrap vessels and anastomosis sites
•
flour for controlling arterial bleeding

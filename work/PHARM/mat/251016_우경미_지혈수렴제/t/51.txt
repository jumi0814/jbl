Anti-Fibrinolysis Agents
Pharmacology & Therapeutics for Dentistry, 7th Ed
Hemostatic Mouthwash
•
A compounded hemostatic mouthwash (tranexamic acid or aminocaproic acid) can be 
used if the patient is unable to hold warfarin. 
•
Written prescription should be for tranexamic acid 5% solution, 100mL.  
•
Instructions:  
Before dental procedure, hold 5 mL in the mouth for 5-10 minutes
Following dental procedure, hold 5~10 mL in the mouth for 1-2 minutes, then spit.  May 
repeat every 2 hours. 
•
Solution is good for 5 days after compounding.  Must be refrigerated.  
처음에는 낮은 concentration을 사용하여 시작하자.

4. Oxygenating agents
➢General Features
• Liberate molecular oxygen by peroxidase
• Have some bactericidal activity, but germicidal activity in oral cavity is negligible
• Organic matters reduce antibacterial activity
• Used as the mechanical cleanser rather than antiseptics
• Fairly unstable and even explosive at high concentration
• Have bleaching action at high concentration
➢Mode of Action
• Hydroxyl free radicals attack membrane lipids, DNA, and other components
• Antibacterial action: directly oxidize the microbial constituents
• Cleansing action: loosen debris in inaccessible areas by formation of bubble & 
foam as molecular oxygen is liberated

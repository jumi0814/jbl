➢Precautions
• Contraindicated in newly regenerating tissues due to tissue 
destruction & irritation
• Should not be used as mouthwash for prolonged periods 
because of tooth decalcification and development of black 
hairy tongue
➢Preparations
• Hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) 30% (Tooth bleaching)
• Diluted hydrogen peroxide 3% (Wound cleaning)
• Diluted hydrogen peroxide 1.5% (Mouth wash)
• Sodium perborate ( [Na⁺]₂·[B₂O₄(OH)₄]2−): widely used 
in laundry detergents, as one of the peroxide-based bleaches
4. Oxygenating agents

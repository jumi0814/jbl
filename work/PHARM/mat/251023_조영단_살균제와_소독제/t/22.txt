5. Hypochlorite substances
➢General Features
• [Sodium or calcium or lithium] + Hypochlorite (OCl-)
• Potent germicidal agents
• Highly reactive with organic matters
• Fairly stable in alkali
• Antibacterial effect is increased with high temperature
• Have solvent action at high concentration
➢Mode of Action
• Free halogens react covalently with key enzyme systems. Iodine and Chlorine are the most 
effective halogens
• Antibacterial action of Hypochlorite is mediated by destructive action of free, elemental form of 
Chlorine and undissociated hydrochlorous acid (HOCl)

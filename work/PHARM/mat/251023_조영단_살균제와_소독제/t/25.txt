6. Phenolic Compounds
➢General Features
•
Bacteriostatic/bactericidal and fungicidal
•
Depending on the concentration of phenolic compound, 
the activity result in disruption of cells, precipitation of cell protein. 
•
Have local anesthetic effect
•
Diffusible and have penetrating ability, but onset is delayed
•
Antibacterial effect is decreased by organic matters, alkali, and low temp.
•
Slightly water-soluble

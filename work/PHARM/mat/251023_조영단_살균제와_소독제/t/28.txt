7. Zinc oxide-Eugenol preparations
➢General Features
• Compatible with both soft and hard tissues
• Has sedative action on the pulpal tissue by analgesic and local  anesthetic effects 
of eugenol
• Mild antiseptic
• When mixed, an acid-base reaction takes place with the formation of long, sheath-
like crystals of chelate compound, zinc eugenolate, and forms a matrix in hardened 
mass
• Reaction is accelerated by heat, moist, zinc acetate, and glacial acetic acid
• Has low thermal conductivity
➢Mode of Action
• Zinc oxide: astringent and antiseptic
• Eugenol (Phenolic): weak antimicrobial activity but rapid analgesic properties

Antiseptics vs. Disinfectants
• Antiseptics (살균제)
: biocides or products that destroy or inhibit the growth of microorganisms
in or on living tissue. (e.g. health care personnel hand washes and surgical scrubs).
• Disinfectants (소독제)
: similar but generally used on inanimate objects or surfaces
Clin Microbiol Rev 1999; 12(1): 147–179.

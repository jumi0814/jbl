• Glutaraldehyde: Cold sterilant (2 - 2.5% glutaraldehyde)
➢Precautions
• Highly irritant and toxic to skin and eye
• Allergenic
• Reduced effect by organic matters (still active in the presence of organic matters)
• Formaldehydes polymerize to paraformaldehyde on standing that can be 
prevented by addition of glycerin as in Formocresol (used in vital pulpotomy of 
primary teeth)
• Mummifying (Fixation) agent..

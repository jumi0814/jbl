1. Alcohols
➢General Features
• Bactericidal against vegetative form
• Inexpensive, easily obtainable
• Has cleaning action and ready to evaporated
• Colorless and non-staining property
➢Mode of Action
• Denaturation of bacterial proteins
• More effective in the presence of water, thus 60-70% prep is commonly used
• Metabolic interference

➢Clinical Uses
• Skin degermination for injection
• Preoperative preparation for surgery
• Vehicle for the other antiseptic agents such as iodine, hexachlorphene etc.
• Preservation of presterilized items for short duration
• Sterilization of dental instruments.
➢Preparations
• Ethyl alcohol (Ethanol): 60-80%        
• Isopropyl alcohol (Isopropanol):  70%
➢Precautions
• Irritation and formation of the coagulum, hence not recommended for open wounds
• Not suitable for the cold sterilization of the dental equipment, whose surfaces are 
leather or plastic
• Flammability

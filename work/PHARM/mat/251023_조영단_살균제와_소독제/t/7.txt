2. Iodine compounds
➢General Features
• Most valuable, powerful antiseptic agent and 
have bactericidal, sporicidal, fungicidal and virucidal effects
• Have relative low toxicity
• Have rapid action, high penetrating ability and active over a wide range of pH
• Slightly soluble in the water, moderately soluble in alcohol and glycerin
• Not affected by presence of organic matters and incompatible with alkali
• Have disclosing activity
➢Mode of Action
• Precipitation of proteins and salt formation by direct halogenation with free or 
liberated iodine
• Partially absorbed, partially loose-bound to tissue, partially converted into iodide and 
continuing the penetration into deep site

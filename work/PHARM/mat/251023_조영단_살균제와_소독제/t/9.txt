3. Bis-biguanides
➢General Features
• Bactericidal against G(+) and G(-) bacteria
• Ineffective against tubercle bacilli, spore and viruses
• Have cationic detergent action
• Activity is not reduced by organic matters
• Currently valuable agent for the control of dental plaque
• Sensitive to light

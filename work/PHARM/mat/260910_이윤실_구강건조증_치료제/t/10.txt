Autonomic Neurotransmitters & Receptors
1. All autonomic nervous system (ANS) 
neurons are cholinergic except post-
ganglionic sympathetic, which is 
adrenergic.
2. Cholinergic neurons release Ach.
3. Adrenergic neurons release NE.
4. Cholinergic receptors: 
• Two majors: Nicotinic and Muscarinic.
5.  Adrenergic receptors: 
• Two majors: Alpha and Beta 
6.  All parasympathetic responses are 
mediated by activation of muscarinic
receptors.

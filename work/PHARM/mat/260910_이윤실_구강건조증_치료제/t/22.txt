Acetylcholine synthesis, storage, release, and degradation pathways, 
and pharmacologic agents that act on these pathways.
1. Cholineis transported into the presynaptic cholinergic nerveterminal 
by a high-affinity Na+-choline co-transporter.
2. The cytosolic enzyme choline acetyltransferase catalyzes the
formation of acetylcholine (ACh)from acetyl coenzyme A
(AcCoA) and choline. Newly synthesized AChis packaged
(together with ATP and proteoglycans) into vesicles forstorage. 
Transport of AChinto the vesicle is mediated by a H+-AChantiporter.
3. The ACh-containing vesicles fuse with the plasmamembrane when 
intracellular calcium levels increase inresponse to a presynaptic action 
potential.
4. Acetylcholinediffuses in the synaptic cleft and binds topostsynaptic 
and presynaptic receptors.
5. Presynaptic nicotinic receptors enhance Ca2+entry into the
presynaptic neuron, thereby increasing vesicle fusion andrelease of 
ACh; presynaptic M2and M4muscarinic receptorsinhibit Ca2+
entry into the presynaptic neuron, therebydecreasing vesicle fusion 
and release of ACh.
6. Acetylcholinein the synaptic cleft is degradedby membrane-bound 
acetylcholinesterase (AChE) into choline and acetate.
- ACh의 acetyl은 acetyl-CoA에서 오고 choline은 밖에서 흡수하는 것 예전에 배웠습니다. 
- nicotinic rec., 홀수 muscarinic rec.은 excitatory, 짝수 muscarinic rec.은 inhibitory입니다. 
- ACh은 AChE 에 의해 분해되어 재활용됩니다. 
- 파란색이 각 단계에 작용하는 약들입니다.

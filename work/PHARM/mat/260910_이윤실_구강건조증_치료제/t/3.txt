Simple Anatomy of Autonomic 
Nervous System (ANS)
➢The ANS is composed entirely of efferent neurons.
➢The ANS consists of a two-neuron outflow:
• Preganglionic axons emanating from cell
bodies in the central nervous system (brain and
spinal cord).
• Postganglionic fibers originating in autonomic
ganglia.

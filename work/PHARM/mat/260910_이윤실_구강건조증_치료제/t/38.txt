Sjögren’s syndrome
• A long-term autoimmune disease that affects the 
body's moisture-producing (lacrimal and 
salivary) glands, and often affects other organs, such 
as the lungs, kidneys, and nervous system.
• Described in 1933 by Henrik Sjögren
(Swedish ophthalmologist).
• 0.2 - 1.2% of the population is affected.
• Females are more affected (~ 10 times).
• Commonly begins in middle age.
• Primary symptoms are dryness (dry mouth & eyes), 
pain and fatigue. Others include dry skin, vaginal 
dryness, a chronic cough, numbness in the arms and 
legs, muscle and joint pains, and thyroid problems.
• An increased risk (5%) of lymphoma.
• The most specific diagnostic test requires lip biopsy.

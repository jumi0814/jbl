Perioperative Anticoagulation and Antithrombotic Management
•
Patients on chronic anti-coagulation often need to undergo procedures that pose risk 
of bleeding. Providing optimal anticoagulation treatment is challenging.
•
The risk of thromboembolic events must be weighed against the risk of surgical 
bleeding. Patient preferences regarding the associated complications must be 
considered.
•
The perioperative risk of thromboembolism is greater than expected from extrapolation 
of annual rates in otherwise stable patients, likely due to a rebound hypercoagulable
state from cessation of anticoagulants and hypercoagulability due to surgical trauma.

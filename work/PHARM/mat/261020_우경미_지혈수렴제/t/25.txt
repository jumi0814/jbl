Anticoagulant and Antiplatelet Agents
•
Prescribed for individuals who are at high risk for or who have had thromboembolic 
events (e.g., blood clots)
-
deep vein thrombosis (DVT) 
-
pulmonary embolism (PE) 
-
non-valvular atrial fibrillation (NVAF)
-
stroke
-
myocardial infarction
•
Adverse effects
-
prolonged bleeding
- thromboembolic event history가 있거나 위험이 있는 
위 그룹에 대해서는 잘 기억해주면 좋겠습니다.

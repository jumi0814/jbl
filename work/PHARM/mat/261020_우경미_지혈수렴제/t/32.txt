Anticoagulant and Antiplatelet Medications and Dental Procedures
Sources: ADA Oral Health Topics (2022); SDCEP Dental Anticoagulant Guidance (2022).
Warfarin and dental procedures — current approach
1. Check bleeding and thromboembolic risk
Confirm indication, comorbidities, recent INR and planned procedure.
2. INR below 4 (SDCEP)
Usually continue warfarin for dental procedures; stage extensive work and use local hemostatic m
easures.
3. INR 4 or higher / uncertain
Defer invasive elective care and contact the anticoagulation service; do not independently reduce 
INR.
4. After treatment
Apply firm pressure, sutures and local agents; provide written postoperative guidance.

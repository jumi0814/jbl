Anticoagulant and Antiplatelet Medications and Dental Procedures
Sources: ADA Oral Health Topics (2022); SDCEP Dental Anticoagulant Guidance (2022).
Do not routinely lower INR before dental treatment
Most routine procedures
Continue warfarin when INR is below 4 (SDCEP); use local hemostasis.
Higher bleeding-risk procedures
Individualize the plan according to operative extent, INR, comorbidities and thrombosis risk.
Warfarin dose changes / bridging
Not routine for dental procedures; consult the anticoagulation team if changes are contemplated.
Key clinical safeguard
Avoid treating historic INR cut-offs as universal thresholds for all operations.

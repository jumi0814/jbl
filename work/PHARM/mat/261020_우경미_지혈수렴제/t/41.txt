Absorbable Gelatin Sponge (Gelfoam)
1) General Features
· absorbable, water-insoluble gelatin-base sponge
· able to absorb and hold blood and other fluids within its interstices
· not disintegrated by relatively rough handling
· withstand dry heat at 149°C for 4 hrs
· completely absorbed by the 4 to 6 weeks without excessive cellular reactions
2) Mode of Action
· more physical than the result of altering the blood clotting mechanism
· provides a physical matrix for platelet adhesion and fibrin clot formation
· hemostatic action is augmented by impregnation with thrombin
3) Clinical Use
· applied to bleeding surfaces to control capillary, venous, and arteriolar bleeding

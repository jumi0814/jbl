Anti-Fibrinolysis Agents
Pharmacology & Therapeutics for Dentistry, 7th Ed
Hemostatic Mouthwash
•
Antifibrinolytic mouthwash (e.g., tranexamic acid) may support local hemostasis while 
anticoagulant therapy is continued. 
•
Example formulation: tranexamic acid 5% mouthwash; verify local availability and 
compounding protocol.  
•
Instructions:  
Use according to a validated local protocol; minimize vigorous rinsing after extraction.
Postoperative dose and frequency depend on the prescribed formulation and clinical 
protocol. 
•
Storage and beyond-use date must follow pharmacy compounding specifications.

Vitamin K: Role and Clinical Use
•
Reduced vitamin K supports γ-carboxylation of factors II, VII, IX, X and proteins C and 
S.
•
Vitamin K deficiency and warfarin-associated excessive anticoagulation are key 
indications.
•
Do not routinely administer vitamin K before dental surgery; decisions depend on INR, 
bleeding risk and indication.
•
Warfarin reversal: oral vitamin K when indicated; major bleeding requires IV vitamin K 
plus 4-factor PCC per emergency protocol.
•
Subcutaneous or intramuscular vitamin K is generally not preferred.
•
Diet: maintain consistent vitamin K intake during warfarin treatment.

Q: CHX의 antibacterial action 과정을 순서대로 쓰고, antiplaque action과 구별하시오.
A: Antibacterial action: disruption of bacterial plasma membrane — a) +ve Charged CHX Molecules → b) −ve charged phosphate groups on microbial cell wall → c) CHX interacts with phosphate group of microbial cell which after the cells osmotic equilibrium → Leakage of adenosine tri phosphate · Nucleic Acid → d) Cell Lysis.

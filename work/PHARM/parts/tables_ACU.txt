#TBL Neuropathic vs Nociceptive pain — 임상 양상 (Goldman-Cecil) | k=ACU | src=[[ACU:18]]
H: Clinical characteristic | Neuropathic pain | Nociceptive pain
R: Etiology | Nerve injury {jb:PN21} | Tissue or potential tissue damage
R: Descriptors | Lancinating, shooting, electrical-like {jb:PN21} | Throbbing, aching, pressure-like
R: Sensory deficits | Frequent (e.g., numbness, tingling, pricking) | Infrequent and in nondermatomal or non-nerve distribution
R: Motor deficits | Neurologic weakness may be present | May have pain-induced weakness
R: Hypersensitivity | Pain frequently evoked with nonpainful (allodynia) or painful (exaggerated response) stimuli | Uncommon except for hypersensitivity in the immediate area of an acute injury
R: Character | Distal radiation common | Distal radiation less common; proximal radiation frequent
R: Paroxysms | Exacerbations common and unpredictable | Exacerbations less common and associated with activity
R: Autonomic signs | Can occur in 1/3 to 1/2 of patients | Uncommon
N: 필기: "어떻게 아프세요?"라고 물어볼 때 description 양상이 많이 다름 · autonomic sign은 nociceptive에선 흔치 않음. 20년 표 빈칸(PN21)은 Etiology·Descriptors 4칸.

#TBL Sensitization — 말초 vs 중추(급성기·후기·신경 손상) | k=ACU | src=[[ACU:7]] [[ACU:10]] [[ACU:12]] [[ACU:15]] [[ACU:24]]
H: 구분 | 부위 | 기전 | 결과
R: Peripheral sensitization {jb:PN02} | nociceptors | PGs — lowering the threshold for activation of nociceptors(필기: voltage-gated sodium ch.의 역치↓) · sensitizing agents → PKC·PKA activation | Primary Hyperalgesia → increased wound sensitivity, prolonged discomfort
R: Central — acute phase | DH of spinal cord | activity-dependent plasticity · Glutamate-activated NMDA receptor plays a key role("wind-up") | Secondary Hyperalgesia → increased incident pain, splinting, prolonged disability
R: Central — late phase | neurons in many areas of CNS | COX-2 begins to be expressed(필기: IL-1 → COX-2 transcription↑ → PG↑) | generalized aches, loss of appetite, changes in mood and sleep cycle

N: 공통 결과 현상 = allodynia(통증이 아닌 감각에 통증) · hyperalgesia(더 크게 느낌). Sensitizing mediators: prostaglandins, histamine, bradykinin, protons, nerve growth factor. NSAID는 COX를 저해해 peripheral과 central sensitization을 모두 저해(p.24 필기). 잘 조절 안 된 급성통증 → neural and glial remodeling → chronic pain(p.15).

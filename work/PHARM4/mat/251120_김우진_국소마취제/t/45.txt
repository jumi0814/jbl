(2) 중추신경계 효과
• Excessive absorption causes restlessness, irritability, convulsion  and eventually 
respiratory depression
• Initially block inhibitory cortical activity leading to excitation and  larger dose depress 
both inhibitory and facilitatory neurons  to produce generalized CNS depression
• Major toxic reaction caused by inadvertent accidental I.V. injection resulting in 
overdose
• Manifestations include the CNS stimulation followed by depression and peripheral 
cardiovascular depression
• To avoid, aspiration and slow injection (longer than 1 min) are required
• Treatment is symptomatic including administration of oxygen or  diazepam for seizures

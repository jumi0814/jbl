Antagonism (Pharmacodynamic)
Scopolamine differs from atropine by virtue of its CNS effects, and is used for 
prevention and treatment of motion sickness.
ACh activates mAChRs in intestinal smooth muscle cells. 
→Increases the frequency and amplitude of contractions
필로카핀을 너무 많이 줘서 침이 질질 흐를 때 아트로핀을 주면 competitive inhibition을 하므로 pharmacodynamic antagonism이다.

Antagonism (Pharmacokinetic)
➢The rate of Absorption of the active drug from the G.I. is reduced.
• Ex 1) The absorption of Ketoconazole was reduced by more than half with Cimetidine
（위산분비
억제제）as a consequence of its poor water solubility which is enhanced in 
acid solution.
• Ex 2) Tetracyclines in the digestive tract can combine with metal ions (e.g., calcium, 
magnesium, aluminum, iron) to form complexes poorly absorbed. Antacids（제산제）
include various preparations that contain aluminum hydroxide, calcium carbonate 
and magnesium compounds.
➢The rate of Distribution is decreased by altered drug binding to plasma protein.
• Ex) Insulin, steroids, and growth hormones can raise albumin levels.
• Cf) The level of free Warfarin is increased by co-administration of Diclofenac.
➢The rate of elimination of active drugs is increased by adjusting Metabolism. 
• Ex) Warfarin activity is decreased when given together with Phenobarbital.
➢The rate of Excretion of the active drug or its metabolites is increased.
• Ex) Drugs that act as weak bases are increasingly excreted through renal filtration as the 
pH of the urine becomes more acidic. →Used for intoxications!
• Cf) Asprin and Probenecid inhibits renal tubular secretion of Penicillins.

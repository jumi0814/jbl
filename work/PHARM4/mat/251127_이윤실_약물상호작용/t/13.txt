Mechanisms of Drug Interactions
1. Pharmaceutical (제약) interactions represent drug incompatibilities of 
a physical or chemical nature.
2. Pharmacokinetic interactions derive from the influence of one drug 
on the absorption, distribution, biotransformation, or excretion of 
another drug.
3. Pharmacodynamic interactions represent modifications in the 
pharmacologic effects of a drug independent of any change in the 
quantitative disposition of that drug.

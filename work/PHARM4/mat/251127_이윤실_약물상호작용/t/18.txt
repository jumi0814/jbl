MAOIs
•
Tyramine is often produced by decarboxylation 
of tyrosine during fermentation or decay. It is 
normally inactivated by MAO in the gut. 
MAOIs block tyramine degradation, and 
increased tyramine causes the release of 
large amounts of stored catecholamines (NA, 
noradrenaline), resulting in headache, 
tachycardia, nausea, hypertension, cardiac 
arrhythmias, and stroke. 
•
Have potential for the hypertensive crisis with 
ingestion of fermented foods and drinks 
containing high tyramine such as aged 
cheese, some wine and beer or sausage.
*NA stands for noradrenaline, 
also known as norepinephrine.

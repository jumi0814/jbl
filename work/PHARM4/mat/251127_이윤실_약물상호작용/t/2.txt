Drug Interactions
• Numerous studies have documented that 
drugs are rarely taken in isolation.
• Drug interactions account for 5-10% of all 
adverse reactions to drugs.
• As a prescriber and administrator of drugs, 
the dentist is responsible for ensuring that 
adverse drug interactions do not arise as a 
consequence of the dentist’s therapeutic 
intervention.

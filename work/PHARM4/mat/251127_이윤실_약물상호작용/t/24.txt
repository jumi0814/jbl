P-glycoprotein Transporter (1)
• It impedes the absorption, permeability, and retention of the drugs, extruding 
them out of the cells (→an efflux membrane transporter):
The role is likely to protect the susceptible organs from toxic compounds, 
preventing them to enter the cytosol and extrude them to the exterior. But, It is 
overexpressed in cancer cells and accountable for obstructing cell internalization 
of chemotherapeutic agents and for developing transporter mediated resistance.
Topology of P-glycoprotein:
• Two transmembrane domains 
(TMDs) form the pump and 
provide the transport mechanism 
• Two nucleotide-binding domains 
(NBDs) hydrolyze ATP to power 
the transport.

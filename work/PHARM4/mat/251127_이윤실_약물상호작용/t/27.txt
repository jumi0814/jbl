➢Cimetidine is a potent cytochrome P450 inhibitor (competitive & reversible 
inhibition of CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP2E1, and CYP3A4) →
prolongs half-life of several drugs: 
Opioids, lidocaine, warfarin (Coumadin, anticoagulant), phenytoin, theophylline 
(Mx for asthma tx), barbiturate, benzodiazepines, propranolol, digitoxin, sildenafil 
(Viagra)
➢Cimetidine inhibits the renal excretion →prolongs half-life of several drugs: 
Metformin (Mx for type II diabetic tx) and procainamide (Mx for arrhythmia tx)
➢Cimetidine reduces the absorption of ketoconazole and itraconazole (which 
require a low pH).

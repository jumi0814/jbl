Drug Interactions used in Pharmacotherapeutics
➢Combinations of drugs are used in therapy to provide enhanced 
effects and to prevent adverse drug reactions.
1. Multidrug therapy for HT, Tb, cancer, pain control, etc.
✓
Hypertension: Combining an ACE inhibitor, a calcium channel 
blocker, and a thiazide diuretic.
✓
Tuberculosis: Standard regimen with isoniazid, rifampin, 
pyrazinamide, and ethambutol.
✓
Cancer: Using multiple chemotherapeutic agents such as 
cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, and paclitaxel as part of 
combination therapy
2. Antidotal use
Multidrug therapy를 언제 이용하냐? 일상에서 굉장히 많이 이용하고 있다. Tuberculosis나 Cancer에도 사용되고, 
pain control에도 acetaminophen + opioid 을 섞어 쓰며, 혈압약도 혈관에 작용하는 약과 이뇨제 등을 섞어 쓴다.

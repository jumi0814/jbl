• At therapeutic doses, acetaminophen is rarely associated with 
other side effects. 
• Acute overdose of acetaminophen can cause hepatic failure, 
metabolic acidosis, coagulopathy, and/or encephalopathy.
• The most serious manifestation of acute overdose is 
hepatotoxicity. Degree of liver damage is directly related with 
ingested drug amounts. 
• Hepatotoxicity results from the formation of a highly reactive 
metabolite, NAPQI (N-acetyl-p-benzoquinone imine):
Normal dose → NAPQI reacts rapidly with glutathione
Overdose → Glutathione is depleted, and NAPQI accumulates 
and results in alkylation of liver proteins and 
cellular injury. When enough liver cells are 
damaged, clinical signs of toxicity, such as nausea 
and jaundice, appear.

Additive effect: Codeine + Acetaminophen
Expert Rev Pharmacoecon Outcomes Res.
2006 Apr;6(2):113-21
❖The FDA recently banned prescription drugs from containing more 
than 325 mg of acetaminophen per dose (in combination products). 
❖In 2012, the FDA suggested, but did not mandate, a maximum daily 
dose of Acetaminophen for adults is 3-4 g with no more than 650 mg 
every 6 hours (in OTC single-ingredient products).

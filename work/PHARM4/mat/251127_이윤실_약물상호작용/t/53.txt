BZD receptor antagonist: Flumazenil 
•
Benzodiazepine antagonists are important 
therapeutic compounds that can reverse the 
effects of BZDs.
•
Used to treat overdose or terminate 
sedative or anesthetic effect of BZDs.
•
Discovered to be competitive antagonist at 
BDZ receptor site.
•
Acts quickly within 1~2 min, peak activity 
within 10 min, and duration about 2 hrs.
BDZ의 antidote: Pharmacodynamic antagonism 이다.

•
Inverse agonists 
(역작용제) bind to the 
same receptor as an 
agonist, but induce an 
effect opposite to that 
of the agonist.
•
Agonists for the 
GABAA receptor (such 
as benzodiazepines) 
create a sedative effect, 
whereas inverse agonists 
have anxiogenic effects 
(for example, Ro15-4513) 
or even convulsive effects 
(certain beta-carbolines).

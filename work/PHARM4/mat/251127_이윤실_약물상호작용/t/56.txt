2. Drugs affecting renin-angiotensin system
➢Drug Interaction:
▪The effectiveness of ACE inhibitors may be compromised by NSAIDs.
→The vasodilation mediated by ACE inhibitors through bradykinin (→
prostaglandin synthesis↑) is diminished because NSAIDs reduce 
prostaglandin synthesis.
▪The risk of hyperkalemia increases significantly when used together with 
K+-sparing diuretics (Spironolactone, Eplerenone, Triamterene, Amiloride etc.) 
or potassium supplements.
→ACE inhibitors reduce angiotensin II levels, which leads to decreased 
aldosterone secretion. Since aldosterone promotes sodium reabsorption 
and potassium excretion, reduced aldosterone results in decreased 
potassium elimination. Therefore, ACE inhibitors themselves can cause 
hyperkalemia.

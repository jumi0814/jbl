Antihypertensive Drugs: Implication for Dentistry
Drug interactions
• Centrally acting α2-agonists(Clonidine, 
Guanfacine, Methyldopa): Reduces sympathetic 
nervous system activity by acting on adrenergic 
receptors (혈압감소, 진정 효과 등 유발). →Can 
cause excessive CNS depression when combined 
with antianxiety agent or other CNS depressants.
• Adrenergic neuronal NE depleting agents such as 
reserpine and guanethidine: Postsynaptic 
adrenergic receptors become hypersensitive → 
Induce supersensitivity to the actions of 
exogenously administered catecholamines.
• Nonselective β-blockers may lead to acute 
hypertensive episode when epinephrine (acting 
on α1, β1, β2 rec) is used in local anesthesia, due 
to unopposed α1-mediated vasoconstriction.
• Chronic NSAIDs therapy (>5 days) antagonize 
antihypertensive action of drugs such as ACE 
inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics, and β-blockers.
Adverse effects
• Orthostatic or postural hypotension: 
Peripheral vasodilators, specifically α-
adrenergic blockers (labetalol), can 
exacerbate postural blood pressure (PBP) 
changes and lead to orthostatic 
hypotension (OH). ACE inhibitors, 
angiotensin-receptor antagonists and beta-
adrenoceptor antagonists with intrinsic 
sympathomimetic activity are less likely to 
worsen OH.
• Xerostomia:
Especially common in patients medicated 
with reserpine and the centrally acting α2-
agonists

Nonselective b-blocker를 복용하고 있는 환자에게 epi가 포함된 마취주사를 할때, acute hypertensive episode를 주의해야 한
다. 일반적으로 epi의 작용은 혈관을 수축하는 a1 receptor와, 혈관을 확장하는 b2 receptor의 효과가 합쳐져 나타나는데, 
nonselective b-blocker를 복용하는 환자의 경우 b2 receptor가 차단되어 a1에 의한 혈관 수축 작용만 나타나므로 혈압이 급격하
게 상승할 수 있는 것이다.

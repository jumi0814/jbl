Drug Interactions of Antibiotics: Warfarin
➢Warfarin has anticoagulation effect by antagonize the cofactor functions 
of vitamin K. Most antibiotics, but especially Metronidazole, Macrolides, 
and other broad-spectrum antibiotics including Chloramphenicol, 
Tetracycline, Neomycin, inhibit vitamin K synthesis by intestinal flora. →
Potentiate the effect of Warfarin. →May cause you to bleed more easily.
➢Metronidazole induces down-regulation of CYP2C9, the primary enzyme 
responsible for S-warfarin metabolism. →Increase plasma level of S-
warfarin.
• Note: Metronidazole does not significantly affect gut flora, so it does not 
interact with warfarin through the ‘antibiotic–gut bacteria–vitamin K’ 
pathway.

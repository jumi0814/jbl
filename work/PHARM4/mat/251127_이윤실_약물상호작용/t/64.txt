Drug Interactions of Antibiotics: Penicillins (1)
➢Cell wall synthesis inhibitors like penicillins become maximally effective 
when the bacteria actively proliferate. Antibacterial activity may be 
antagonized by combined bacteriostatic antibiotics such as tetracycline.
➢Aspirin and probenecid may increase the serum half-lives of the 
penicillins by decreasing their renal excretion.
➢Penicillins + Aminoglycosides:
• Synergistic effect: penicillins alter permeability of the bacteria → facilitate 
entry of other antibiotics
• They should never be placed in the same infusion fluid as (+) charged 
aminoglycosides form an inactive complex with (-) charged penicillins.

Drug Interactions of Antibiotics: Macrolides
• GI distress:  most common, especially with EM which binds to 
the motilin receptor. Epigastric pain, abdominal cramps, nausea etc.
❖Motilin: a peptide hormone that stimulates GI motility.
• Cholestatic (obstructive) jaundice: especially with estolate form of EM
• Ototoxicity: transient deafness with high dose EM
• Myopathy: concomitant use of EM or Clarithromycin with statin (CYP3A4 
substrate) due to inhibition of CYP3A4 (not Azithromycin).
• QT interval prolongation: ventricular tachyarrhythmia.
• Pyloric stenosis in infants.
• EM, Azithromycin:  FDA pregnancy category B
Clarithromycin:  FDA pregnancy category C
CYP3A4 inhibitors may 
increase the plasma 
concentration of statins, 
increasing the risk of 
adverse reactions 
such myopathy and/or 
rhabdomyolysis.

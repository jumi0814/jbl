Antagonism (Chemical)
• Dimercaprol, also called British anti-Lewisite (BAL), is a medication used to treat 
acute poisoning by arsenic (비소), mercury, gold, and lead.
• Lewisite is a vesicant arsenic war gas that was used in World War I as a chemical 
weapon (extremely toxic). *A vesicant (발포제/수포제) is a chemical that causes 
severe skin and eye irritation and burns, resulting in painful blisters.

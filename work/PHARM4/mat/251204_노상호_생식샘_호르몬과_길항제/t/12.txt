Medical applications 
• Hormone replacement therapy 
 
   Contraindication: Among the older postmenopausal 
women, an orally-administered estrogen supplement 
has been associated with an increased risk of 
dangerous blood clotting.  
 
   Since estrogen applied topically have a different 
spectrum of side-effects than when administered orally, 
transdermal estrogens do not affect clotting as they 
are absorbed directly into the systemic circulation, 
avoiding first-pass metabolism in the liver. This route 
of administration is thus preferred in women with a 
history of thrombo-embolic disease.

간을 통과하며 나오는 중간산물에 의해 나타나는 현상이며, Transdermal로 투여하는 방법으로 부작용을 예방할 수 있다.

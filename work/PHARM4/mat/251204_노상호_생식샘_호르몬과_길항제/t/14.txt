Medical applications 
• Under certain circumstances, used in males for 
treatment of prostate cancer. 
 
• Estrogen-only products (without progestagen) may 
increase the risk of uterine bleeding and 
endometrial cancer.  
 
• Anti-estrogen therapy: hormone-receptor-positive 
breast cancers are treated with drugs which 
suppress production in the body of estrogen. 
Certain foods such as soy may also suppress the 
effects of estrogen and are used as an alternative to 
hormone therapy.

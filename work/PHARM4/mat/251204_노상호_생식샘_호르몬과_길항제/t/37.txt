Early postnatal effects 
•
The first visible effects of rising androgen 
levels in childhood, and occur in both boys 
and girls in puberty. 
 
1.
Adult-type body odor  
2.
Increased oiliness of skin and hair, acne  
3.
Pubarche (appearance of pubic hair)  
4.
Axillary hair  
5.
Growth spurt, accelerated bone maturation  
6.
Fine upper lip and sideburn hair

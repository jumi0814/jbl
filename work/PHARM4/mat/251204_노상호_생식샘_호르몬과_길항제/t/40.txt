Effects on the brain 
•
The brain is also affected by this "sexual" advancement; 
the enzyme aromatase converts testosterone into 
estrogen that is responsible for masculinization of the 
brain in a male fetus. 
 
•
There are some differences in a male and female brain 
(the result of different testosterone levels); a clear 
difference is the size, the male human brain is on 
average larger, however in females (that do not use 
testosterone as much) the corpus callosum is 
proportionally larger.  
      This means that the effect of testosterone is a 
greater overall brain volume, but a decreased 
connection between the hemispheres.

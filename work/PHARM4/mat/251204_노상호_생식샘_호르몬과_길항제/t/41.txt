Effects on the brain 
• Recent research has suggested that alcohol 
consumption in women can temporarily raise 
testosterone levels.  
 
• A 2006 BBC article states: "According to medical 
research, testosterone - the hormone connected 
to male characteristics such as aggression and 
sex drive - rises in women by up to 50% when 
they get drunk. In men, it falls" 
알코올은 여성에서 testosterone level을 높인다고 알려져 있다. 남자에게서는 상관관계를 크게 보이지 않는다고 한다.

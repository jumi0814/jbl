17
Recommendations for codeine therapy
Implications
Phenotype
Avoid codeine use because of potential for serious 
toxicity. If opioid use is warranted, consider a non-
tramadol opioid.
Increased enzyme activity; Increased 
formation of morphine leading to 
higher risk of toxicity.
UM
Use codeine label-recommended age- or weight-
specific dosing.
Normal enzyme activity; Expected 
morphine formation.
NM
Use codeine label-recommended age- or weight-
specific dosing. If no response, and opioid use is 
warranted, consider a non-tramadol opioid.
Intermediate enzyme activity; 
Reduced morphine formation.
IM
Avoid codeine use because of possibility of 
diminished analgesia. If opioid use is warranted, 
consider a non-tramadol option.
Absent enzyme activity; Greatly 
reduced morphine formation following 
codeine administration, leading to 
insufficient pain relief.
PM
Tramadol이 아닌 non-tramadol opioid를 선택하라는 것은 tramadol 또한 
2D6에 의해 대사되는 약물이란 것. 
2D6의 Ultrarapid metabolizer의 경우 codeine을 사용하지 않는 것이 좋겠다.
반대로 poor metabolizer의 경우 효과가 없을 수 있다.

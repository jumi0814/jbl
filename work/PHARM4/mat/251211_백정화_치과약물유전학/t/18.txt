• Tramadol Therapy and CYP2D6 Genotype
ü Tramadol is an analgesic used to treat moderate to severe pain. 
§
A centrally acting MOR agonist with weak inhibitory activity on the reuptake of NE and 5-HT.
§
Used for a variety of pain conditions, including post-operative pain, cancer pain, and 
musculoskeletal pain
ü The CYP2D6 enzyme converts tramadol to the active metabolite, O-desmethyltramadol, 
which is up to 6 times more potent than tramadol in producing analgesia.
ü Life-threatening respiratory depression and death have occurred in children who 
received tramadol, and in at least one case, the child was an UM of tramadol.
18
Opioid agonist로 작용하지만, 동시에 TCA와 유사하게 NE와 5-HT의 reuptake의 inhibition 해서 pain
이 CNS로 전달되는 pathway에서 inhibitory signal을 전달하도록 한다. Tramadol 또한 
demethylation 과정을 통해 만들어진 산물이 opioid receptor에 작용하는데, 이때 2D6가 작용한다.

• Cevimeline Therapy and CYP2D6 Genotype
ü Cevimeline, an M1 and M3 agonist, is used for symptomatic tx. of dry mouth in patients 
with Sjögren syndrome. 
ü Cevimeline 30 mg is taken orally, three times a day, 30 min before each meal.
ü CYP2D6, CYP3A3 and CYP3A4 are responsible for the metabolism of cevimeline.
ü Should be used with caution in CYP2D6 PM as they may be at a higher risk of adverse 
events.
ü Patients with increased risk of adverse responses include those with asthma, COPD, 
peptic ulcer, and GI or urinary tract obstructions.

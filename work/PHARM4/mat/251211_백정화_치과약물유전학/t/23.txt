• Diazepam Therapy and CYP2C19 Genotype
ü Approximately 3~5% of Caucasians, 13~15% of East Asians, and as much as 57% of 
Oceanians have reduced or absent CYP2C19 enzyme activity (PM).
ü Diazepam have reduced clearance and increased elimination half-lives in geriatric 
populations.
ü There is a potentially relevant drug interaction between diazepam (and midazolam) 
and compounds which inhibit CYP3A and CYP2C19 that may lead to increased and 
prolonged sedation.

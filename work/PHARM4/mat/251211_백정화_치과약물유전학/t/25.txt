• NSAIDs Therapy and CYP2C9 Genotype
ü CYP2C9 metabolizes approximately 15% of drugs, including NSAIDs and the narrow 
therapeutic index drugs, warfarin and phenytoin.
ü The most commonly described CYP2C9 alleles are *2 and *3, which result from 
nonsynonymous SNPs that lead to decreased enzyme function. 
ü Approximately 30% of the White population have a *2 or *3 allele while approximately 6% of the Black 
and about 6% to 8% of the Asian carry them. 
ü Decreased CYP2C9 activity may increase the risk for GI bleeding and other adverse 
events particularly for NSAIDs with a longer half-life, such as celecoxib, flurbiprofen, 
ibuprofen, meloxicam, and piroxicam. 
ü The CPIC recommends initiating a lower dose of these drugs or choosing alternative 
therapy for patients with genotypes that reduce enzyme activity.

• Carbamazepine Therapy and HLA Genotype
ü Carbamazepine (Tegretol) is often used to treat epilepsy and bipolar disorder and to 
relieve pain in trigeminal neuralgia.
ü Hypersensitivity reactions can occur in up to 10% of patients, and typically affect the 
skin. 
ü Some of these reactions are mild, as in the case of maculopapular exanthema; however, 
conditions such as Stevens–Johnson syndrome (SJS), toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), 
and drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms (DRESS) are potentially 
life-threatening.
ü Over 90% of Tegretol treated patients who will experience SJS/TEN have this reaction 
within the first few months of treatment.

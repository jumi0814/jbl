Management of the pregnant dental patients
• Avoid elective dental tx. except in the 2nd / earlier part of the 3rd trimester.
• Avoid all drugs (especially during the 1st trimester) unless potential benefit outweighs potential risks.
• If drugs are needed, check the categories to choose the safest.
• Minimize periodontal problems: oral prophylaxis before pregnancy or during 2nd trimester to prevent 
low-birth-weight newborns.
• Avoid radiographs unless absolutely necessary; use lead apron.
• If morning sickness is a problem, schedule an afternoon appointment.
• Position pt. in recumbent position in last trimester with right hip elevated to prevent supine hypotension 
syndrome.
• Give frequent breaks for urination, especially during 3rd trimester.

Digital subtraction radiography
• A technique for diagnosing subtle changes in 
radiographic density in serial radiographs of 
the same anatomical region
• For early detection of disease  
• For measurement of disease progression 
• By diagnosis and monitoring from a 
longitudinal evaluation of changes over time   
두 영상의 차이를 만들어서(=subtraction) 이런 것들을 볼 수 있음
1) early state
2) 질환의 진행상태를 측정
3) 치료 과정 관찰

Digital subtraction radiography
• For eliminating unnecessary anatomic 
structures on a radiographic image 
• Using the pre- and postprocedure images in a 
computer 
• Display the final subtracted image by 
combining them together digitally
• Emphasizes the differences between the two 
original images

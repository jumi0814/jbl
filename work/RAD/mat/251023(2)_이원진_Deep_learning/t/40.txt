Clinical Applications and Significance
• Enhanced Diagnostic Accuracy and 
Consistency: more consistent and 
objective diagnoses, reducing inter-
observer variability
• Reduced Diagnostic Burden in Complex 
Cases: differentiating between lesions 
such as OKCs and ameloblastomas, 
offering valuable second-opinion 
support
• Support for General Dental 
Practitioners: computer-aided 
diagnostic (CAD) tool supporting less-
experienced clinicians in identifying 
lesions   
• Efficient Screening in Large-Scale 
Dental Exams: quickly flag potential 
pathologies for follow-up, increasing 
efficiency

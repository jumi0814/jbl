Comparison of 2D, 2.5D, and 3D segmentation networks for 
maxillary sinuses and lesions in CBCT image
Clinical Applications and Significance
• 2.5D networks using ensemble learning 
across orthogonal planes (axial, sagittal, 
coronal) show superior segmentation 
accuracy
• Allow for automated, consistent 
segmentation, reducing workload and 
increasing reproducibility in diagnosis and 
treatment planning
• Making it feasible for clinical application 
replacing manual segmentation of sinus 
structures, time-consuming and subjective
• Enhances surgical safety, diagnostic 
efficiency, and treatment predictability in 
implant dentistry—especially in patients 
requiring sinus lift procedures
• Applicability in General and Specialized 
Dental Practice

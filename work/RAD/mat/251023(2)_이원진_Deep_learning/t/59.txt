Reproduction of Natural Head Position 
•Accurate determination of the 
natural head position (NHP): 
essential in orthognathic surgery for 
optimal surgical planning and 
improved patient outcomes
•Natural Head Position (NHP) serves 
as a fundamental reference for 
planning and assessing outcomes in 
orthognathic surgery
•Elimination of External Devices and 
Manual Variability: traditional 
methods for determining NHP often 
require external devices (e.g., bite 
jigs, fiducial markers) or patient 
cooperation, which can introduce 
error and discomfort
NHP-Net
• NHP-Net, Geometric deep learning 
network to 3D skull point clouds:  
automatic reproduction of NHP from CT 
scans, improving the reliability of 
preoperative orientation and surgical 
simulation 
• NHP-Net operates solely on CT data, 
eliminating the need for additional 
equipment and reducing clinical burden 
and patient discomfort by replacing 
manual and device-dependent 
techniques
• NHP-Net: accurate diagnosis, 
standardized treatment planning, and 
enhanced surgical outcomes in 
orthognathic and craniofacial surgery
Geometric deep learning network 
(NHP-Net)

DCrownFormer: Morphology-aware Point-to-Mesh Generation 
Transformer for Dental Crown Prosthesis 
Clinical Applications and Significance
• Automates the generation of anatomically 
accurate dental crowns from 3D scan data
• Enhance clinical workflows by fast,
personalized, and high-quality prosthesis 
design, improving both treatment efficiency 
and patient outcomes
• Reduction of Clinical Time and Human Error: 
reduce manual design time, inter-operator 
variability, and the human error, enabling 
faster and more consistent restorations
• Scalability to Other Types of Prostheses:  
extensible to other prosthetic types such as 
inlays, onlays, and bridges
• Seamless Integration into Digital Dentistry 
Workflows: compatible with IOS data and 
CAD/CAM pipelines, supporting same-day 
restorations and chairside manufacturing, 
enhancing patient convenience and clinic 
efficiency

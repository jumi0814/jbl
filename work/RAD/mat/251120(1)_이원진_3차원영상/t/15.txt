scenes and to output this as a structure
system
◼Partition the image volume into different 
regions, which are homogeneous with 
respect to some formal criteria
◼Binary(hard) segmentation: voxel belongs to a 
certain region is yes or no
◼fuzzy segmentation: a set of probabilities is 
assigned to every voxel

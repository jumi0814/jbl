Preprocessing
◼Volume of interest
◼Converts a given scene to another scene by reducing the 
size of the scene domain and/or the intensity range for 
the purpose of minimizing the storage space
◼Filtering
◼Converts a given scene to another scene by suppressing 
unwanted information and/or enhancing wanted 
information
◼Interpolation
◼Converts a given scene to another scene of a specified 
level of discretization
- volume of interest: 관심있는 특정 부분만을 추출하는 것
- filtering: 원하는 부분만을 enhance시키고, 원하지 않는 것을 suppression함
- interpolation: 해상도를 높이는 것

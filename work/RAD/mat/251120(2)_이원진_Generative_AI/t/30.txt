DCrownFormer+
• End-to-end transformer model to 
perform morphology-aware crown 
generation and refinement
• Implicit Grid Refinement Network 
(IGRN) and two loss functions: to 
generate high-fidelity and realistic 
dental crown meshes by refining the 
details of the coarse indicator grid
• Significant improvements over 
previous approaches: in shape 
completeness, surface smoothness, 
and functional fitting with adjacent 
and antagonist teeth.
DCrownFormer+
Clinical Applications and Significance
• Automatic, consistent, and high-fidelity 
crown generation: significantly reducing 
dentist workload and inter-operator 
variability
• Efficiency and Real-Time Potential: 
chairside applications and integration 
into digital dentistry workflows, including 
web- and cloud-based platforms
• Precision and Functional Quality: 
improvements in penetration distance, 
positioning, and functional quality 
compared to conventional methods
• Applicability in 3D Printing and CAD/CAM: 
compatibility with current CAD/CAM and 
rapid chairside restorations
• Fully automated digital prosthodontics:  
potentially lowers the barrier for clinics 
with limited CAD expertise

MeshFormer: Automatic Alignment of Teeth for Orthodontic 
Treatment
Clinical Applications and Significance
• Streamlining the orthodontic planning 
process
• Use of a shape-aware loss function—taking 
into account all tooth surfaces, center of 
mass, and anterior arch structure—ensures 
clinically meaningful alignment outcomes
• Support for Personalized and Efficient 
Orthodontic Simulation: allow for 
individualized transformation of 
malocclusion into an ideal occlusion
• Assisting both orthodontists and patients: 
automated simulation and visualization in 
treatment planning and communication 
• Potential Reduction in Clinical Workload 
and Diagnostic Variability: to reduce 
orthodontists’ workload, increase planning 
efficiency, and minimize inter-clinician 
variability

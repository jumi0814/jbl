2. [서술형] Maxillary artery의 1st, 2nd, 3rd 
part를 나누는 경계 및 각각에 대한 br.를
서술하시오.
(각 part당 br 4개 이상 적으면 만점) (20,19)
답) 
1st part (mandibular part): origin ~ post. border of 
lat. pterygoid m.
- Deep auricular a.
- Ant. tympanic a.
- Middle meningeal a.
- Accessory meningeal a.
- Inf. Alveolar a.
2nd part (pterygoid part): post. border of lat. 
pterygoid m. ~ pterygopalatine fossa
- Masseteric a.
- Deep temporal a.
- Pterygoid br.
- Buccal a.
3rd part (pterygopalatine part): in the 
pterygopalatine fossa
- Post. sup. alveolar a.
- Infraorbital a.
- A. of pterygoid canal
- Pharyngeal br.
- Descending palatine a.
- Sphenopalatine a.
참고) 210914, 이종호, vascular anatomy, p.28-29

답) [Sinus arterial supply]
1) IOA : Infraorbital artery
2) MA : Maxillary artery

4) IA : Intraosseous branch of PSAA

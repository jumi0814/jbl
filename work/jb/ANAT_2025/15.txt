9. Masseter m, medial pterygoid m, lateral 
pterygoid m, buccinator m 의 기능을 설명하라. 
(23,22)
답)
1. Masseter m.
- Mouth closing
- Protrusion of mandible 
2. Medial pterygoid m.
- Elevates mandible, closes jaw, helps lateral 
pterygoids in moving the jaw from side to side
3. Lateral pterygoid m.
- Depresses and protrude mandible, side to side 
movement of mandible
4. Buccinator m.
- Compresses the cheek against the teeth and 
used in acts such as blowing
- Assistant muscle of mastication(chewing) and in 
neonates it is used to suckle

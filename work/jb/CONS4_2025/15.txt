35. Dycal 과 MTA 의 한계점에 대해 서술하시오. 
(24 탈)
답:
[Dycal(수산화칼슘)의 한계점]
- Presence of tunnels in dentin barrier
- Extensive dentin formation
- High solubility in oral fluids
- Lack of adhesion
- Degradation after acid etching
[MTA 의 한계점]
- Long initial setting time
- Lack of cohesive property after mixed with water
- Wash-out tendency
참고: 241203_손원준_Current Updated Concept of 
Vital Pulp Therapy_p.54,61
36. 천공에 대해서 옳은 것은? (24 탈)

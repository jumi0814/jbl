<Indications>
1. Adequate Canal filling
2. Pulp remnants, residual tissue in the pulp horn, 
filling material, or medicaments
3. Internal hemorrhage following trauma (necrosis 
-> degradation products)
<Contraindications>
1. Extensively restored teeth
2. Cracks and hypoplastic or severely undermined 
enamel
3. Discoloration from metallic salts

답: 
* Protocol for non-vital bleaching (NVB)
1) Perform examination
- Photographs
- Pulp test, palpation, percussion, transillumination
- Radiographs
- Evaluation of pervious restorations and caries
2) Prophylaxis
3) Determine CEJ
4) Protect with rubber dam
5) Debride
- Endodontic obturation 
- Pulpal remnants and debris
- Remove 2-3mm of obturating material from 
root canal
6) Fill
- Cement seal over gutta percha against leakage 
of bleaching agent
- Following CEJ & 1mm incisal
- Minimum 2mm (LC GIC)
7) Mix sodium perborate and 3% H2O2 (water)
8) Seal with 2mm thick caviton, LC-GIC
9) Recall check in 1week repeat as needed

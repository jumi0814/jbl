14. Nonanatomical tooth 장점은? ★ (20,19) 
답: 
1) do not lock the mandible in one position
2) permit the use of simple, less time consuming 
technique and articulators.
3) minimize horizontal pressures - less damage
4) centric relation can be used as an area rather 
than a point
5) adapts easily to class II & class III jaw relations
6) easier to arrange in crossbite

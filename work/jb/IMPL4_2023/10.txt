13. 2가지 bone healing에 대해 서술하시오.
답:
1) Direct bone healing 
• mechanism of bone healing seen when 
there is no motion at graft or fracture site 
(i.e. rigid internal fixation)
• does not involve formation of fracture 
callus
• osteoblast originate from endothelial and 
perivascular cells
• A cutting cone is formed that crosses the 
graft or fracture site
• Osteoblasts lay down lamellar bone 
behind the osteoclasts forming a 
secondary osteon
• Gradually the fracture or the gap is healed 
by the formation of numerous secondary 
osteons
• Contact healing 혹은 gap healing
2) Indirect bone healing
• mechanism for healing in fractures that 
are not rigidly fixed. 
• bridging periosteal (soft) callus and 
medullary (hard) callus re-estblaish 
structural continuity
• callus subsequently undergoes 
endochondral ossification
참고: 210928_이종호_골이식 생리 및 원리 p.23-32

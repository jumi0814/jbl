8. Branemark 와 Cochran 각각 입장의
osseointegration 의 정의를 쓰시오.
답: 해설 참조
참고: 250930_김성균_임플란트 보철 치료의 이해
p.10
해설:
'Definition of Osseointegration by branemark'
1) Bone-to-implant contact at the light 
microscopic level
2) Direct structural and functional connection 
between ordered living bone and the surface of 
a load-carrying implant
'New definition of Osseointegration by Chocran'
1) Stability of an implant in bone that 
represents a dynamic equilibrium between 
exisiting native bone(primary contact) and 
remodeling and new bone formation(secondary 
contact)
2) It's maintenance at the bone-implant 
interface
곧, branemark 의 초기 osseointegration 은 골과
임플란트의 '접촉'만을 뜻하나 Chocran 의 수정된
정의로는 초기에 존재했던 골과 remodeling 을
통해 형성된 신생골 간의 동적 평형 상태입니다.

10. Basic principle of implant occlusion 에
대해 기술하시오.
답:
1. Bilateral stability in centric(habitual) 
occlusion
 - Beyron(1969)
2. Evenly distributed occlusal contacts and 
force
3. No interference between retruded position 
and centric(habitual) position
4. Wide freedom in centric (habitual occlusion)
 - Weinberg(1998)
5. Anterior guidance whenever possible
 - Gibbs et al. (1981), Quirynen et al. (1992)
6. Smooth, even, lateral excursive movements 
without working/non-working interferences
 - Chapman(1989), Engelman(1996)

< Immediate implant: advantages >
1) Shortened treatment time
2) Bone volume maintenance: marginal bone 
preservation
3) Good esthetic results: enhance soft tissue 
support
4) Fewer surgical sessions 
5) May not need flap raising
6) Immediate provisional restoration
< Immediate implant: disadvantages >
1) Increased risk of Infection and failure
2) Mismatch between implant surface to socket 
wall
3) Difficult implant stability
4) Necessity of raising flap (2 stage implant)

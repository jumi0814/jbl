해설:
Immediate occlusal loading은 식립 시 초기
안정성을 확보한 임플란트에 식립한 후 즉시
임시보철물을 제작하여 기능을 부하하는 개념(48-
72 시간 내에 교합을 적용하는 방식)입니다.
다음과 같은 장단점이 있습니다.
Advantages:
1) Less discomfort for patients
- Decreased in discomfort and morbidity
- No second surgery -> fewer appointments
- No removable prosthesis
2) Faster treatment
3) More ideal soft tissue drape
4) Immediate satisfaction and patient acceptance
- Improvement in esthetics
- Maintenance in mastication and muscle mass
5) Greater bone-implant contact
Disadvantages:
1) Increased skill level required
2) Initial longer appointment duration: 
surgical/prosthetic
- Exceeding the patient’s tolerance for 
appointment length
- Increased possibility of medical complications
3) Possible increased implant morbidity
- Implant bone loss or implant failure, but not 
supported by clinical studies and research
- Patient’s loss of confidence in the doctor,

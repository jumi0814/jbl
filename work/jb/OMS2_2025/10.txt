9. 피지샘 모근 땀샘 위치 그려진 그림과 깊이에
따른 설명이 주어진 객관식 문항(22)
답: 해설참고
참고: 251124_박주영_연조직이식 p.18-19
해설:
각 층의 특징과 해당하는 조직은 다음과
같습니다.
1) Epidermis: contains cells that produce 
pigment and protect immune system 
- Hair shaft, pore of sweat gland duct 
2) Dermis: contains nerve endings, oil and sweat 
glands, and hair follicles
- Hair follicle, sebaceous gland, 
nerve ending (Pacinian corpuscle) 
3) Subcutaneous tissue: made up of fat, 
connective tissue, and larger blood vessels
= hypodermis
- Adipose tissue, cutaneous vascular plexus

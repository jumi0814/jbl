12. 악교정수술 시 과두의 골편이 잘못 위치되어
condylar sag 이 생기면 어떻게 되는가?(23)
답:
1) non-contact condyle sag
(1) Skeletal elapse
- Reseating immediatae to post.op 8wks.
- Apparent soon after surgery, 
unless it is masked by intermaxillary fixation,
class II elastics, or habituation 
2) Contact condyle sag
(1) post. condylar compression 
- retrodiscal tissues compressed against the 
posterior fossa wall
(2) lateral condylar torque
(3) medial condylar torque
→ skeletal and dental relapse (late relapse)
참고: 241114_최진영 pf_Orthognathic surgery 
and facial bone reconstrction using FACEGIDE 
System p.14-18

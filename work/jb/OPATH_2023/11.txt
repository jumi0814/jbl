해설:
[Foreign-body giant cell]
- A collection of fused macrophages (giant cell) 
which are generated in response to the presence 
of a large foreign body
- The nuclei are arranged in a disorganized 
manner. The nuclei in this cell are centrally 
placed and overlap each other.

_Inflammation of Jaw p28
해설: (이전 자료 해설 포함)
<cervicofacial actinomycosis>
- typical endogenous infection
- history of trauma (teeth extraction or a blow to 
the jaw)
- acute swelling, fistula
- no fever
- the slow growing Actinomyces species induce a 
granulomatous type reaction.
- discharge of pus contains visible granules which 
are yellow and are known as sulphur granules
- agents: Actinomyces israelli
 Arachnia propria
 Actinobacillus actinomycetemcomitans
 Nocardia spp.

Osteoma
• mature compact or cancellous
bone 으로 구성된 benign tumor
• craniofacial skeleton에 국한 / rare
• maxillary and fronto-ethmoidal
sinuses.
• long bones - osteochondroma.
• Clinical & radiographic features
• periosteal & endosteal
• young adult / asymptomatic /
solitary lesion
• circumscribed sclerotic mass

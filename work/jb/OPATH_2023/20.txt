해설: 
Gardner syndrome
• Autosomal dominant inheritance
• chrom.#: 5q21-22
• gene: adenomatous polyposis coli (APC)
• familial colorectal polyposis
• Multiple osteomas of the jaws as well as of long 
bones and skull
• multiple adenomatous polyps of the large 
intestine
• multiple epidermoid cysts of skin
• desmoid (fibrous) tumors of skin
• impacted supernumerary and permanent teeth
• potentially-malignant adenomatous polyps**
1) Familial polyposis Coli(FPC)
2) Gardner’s syndrome
3) Turcot’s syndrome
<clinical & radiographic features of Gardner 
syndrome – bone tumors>
- osteomas of the skull (mandible) and long bones
- radiopaque mass projecting from the surface of 
bone
- jaw, frontal bone, sinus에 흔함 / 다른 증상보다
먼저 나타남.
- multiple odontoma, supernumerary & impacted 
teeth

- The osteomas in Gardner syndrome patients are 
usually noted during puberty and precede the 
development of, or any symptoms from, the bowel 
polyps.

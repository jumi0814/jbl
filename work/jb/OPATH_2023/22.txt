8. Aneurysmal bone cyst vs. Simple bone cyst 
(21)
답: 해설 참조 (짤이라고 복원되어있으나 JB에서 찾을 수
없었습니다)
해설:
[Simple bone cyst]
• Relatively common disease
• Trauma-hemorrhage theory
• Association with cemento-osseous dysplasia
• Clinical and radiographic features
• 10~20세 (35세 이전)
• 주로 mandible, male 60%
• Painless swelling of affected areas / 
History of trauma(?)
• Molar & premolar area
• Radiograph: Well-delineated radiolucent 
defect / 1~10 cm
• Scalloping between teeth
• Thinning of cortex – no cortical reaction
• Diagnosis
• Surgical exploration may be necessary
• No tissue recovered from surgery
• Empty (1/3) or serosanguinous fluid (2/3)
• Histopathologic features
• Thin fibrous wall / resorption lacunae
• Treatment & prognosis
• New bone formation이 surgery 이후 나타
남
• Biopsy는 필요
[Aneurysmal bone cyst]
• Long bone이나 vertebra에 흔함 (30세 이전)
• 악골에는 매우 드물다
• Clinical & radiographic feature
• 20세 이전 / 하악골 / 구치부
• Facial swelling (develop rapidly) / pain
• Radiograph – unilocular or multilocular 
radiolucency
• Marked cortical expansion or thinning
• Severe bleeding upon surgery

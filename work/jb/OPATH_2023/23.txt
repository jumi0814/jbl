[Fibrous dysplasia]
Clinical & radiographic features
Monostotic
• Jaw – most common / 20세 이전, 그 이후에 발견
되기도
• Painless swelling, slow growing mass / more 
common in maxilla
• Radiograph – ‘ground glass’ radiopacity with illdefined border
• Early lesion radiolucent or mottled
• Maxilla – increased radiodensity of skull base
Polyostotic
• Long bone lesion / Pathologic fracture / Pain, 
deformity
• Leg length discrepancy (Hockey stick deformity)
McCune-Albright syndrome
• Café au lait spot – Trunk & thigh
• Endocrine symptom – Sexual precocity (esp 
female)
Histopathologic features
• Irregularly shaped immature bone trabecula in 
fibrous stroma
• Curvilinear shape (Chinese character) / 
metaplastic bone
• Jaw lesion tend to mature (not in long bone) -> 
more ossified, lamella bone
[Cemento-osseous dysplasia]
Clinical & radiographic features
• Lower anterior teeth / vital tooth
• Marked female predilection (14:1) / 30-50세
• Early lesion radiolucent / End-stage – radiopaque 
with radiolucent rim
• 1 cm 이하의 self-limiting growth
Histopathologic features
• Early – Fibroblastic proliferation with small foci 
of bone formation
• Later – Bone or cementum-like mass deposition

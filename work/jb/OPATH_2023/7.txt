해설:
[Gardner syndrome]
- Autosomal dominant inheritance #5q21-22
- APC gene, familial colorectal polyposis
- Multiple osteoma of jaws as well as long bones 
and skull
- Multiple adenomatous polyps of large intestine
- Multiple epidermoid cysts of skin
- Desmoid (fibrous) tumors of skin
- Impacted supernumerary and permanent teeth
- Potentially malignant adenomatous polyps
-> FPC, Gardner’s syndrome, Turcot’s syndrome
[Clinical & Radiographic features – Bone tumors]
- Osteomas of the skull (mandible) & long bones
- Radiopaque mass projecting from the surface 
of bone
- Jaw, frontal bone, sinus에 흔함 / 다른 증상보다
먼저 나타남.
- Multiple odontoma, supernumerary & impacted 
teeth
[Clinical & Radiographic – Soft tissue tumors]
- Desmoid tumor, fibromas, lipomas, 
neurofibromas, trichilemmoma
- Epidermoid cysts occur in 63-100%, involve 
skull, face, arms, legs, testes, usually antedate 
appearance of colonic polyps

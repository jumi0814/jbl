답: osteoma
참고: 251024_이재일_Benign Nonodontogenic 
Tumors of the Jaws 7~9p
해설: 
Osteoma
• mature compact or cancellous
bone 으로 구성된 benign tumor
• craniofacial skeleton 에 국한 / rare
• maxillary and fronto-ethmoidal
sinuses.
• long bones - osteochondroma.
• Clinical & radiographic features
• periosteal & endosteal
• young adult / asymptomatic /
solitary lesion
• circumscribed sclerotic mass

답: Cemento-osseous dysplasia
참고: 
251024_이재일_Benign Nonodontogenic Tumors 
of the Jaws 42~44p
해설: Periapical COD
- Lower anterior teeth/ vital tooth
- Marked female predilection(4:1)

3. Aneurysmal bone cyst vs. Simple bone cyst 
(21)
답: 해설 참조 (짤이라고 복원되어있으나 JB 에서 찾을
수 없었습니다)
해설:
[Simple bone cyst]
• Relatively common disease
• Trauma-hemorrhage theory
• Association with cemento-osseous dysplasia
• Clinical and radiographic features
• 10~20 세 (35 세 이전)
• 주로 mandible, male 60%
• Painless swelling of affected areas / 
History of trauma(?)
• Molar & premolar area
• Radiograph: Well-delineated radiolucent 
defect / 1~10 cm
• Scalloping between teeth
• Thinning of cortex – no cortical reaction
• Diagnosis
• Surgical exploration may be necessary

[3.Oral Potentially Malignant Disorders :윤혜정]
5. OPMD 정의를 서술하고 가장 빈번한 병소의
임상명과 조직병리학적 특징을 서술하시오. 
(20,22,23,24)
답:
정의 : heterogenous group of clinically defined 
conditions associated with a variable risk of 
progression to oral squamous cell carcinoma
가장 빈번한 병소의 임상명 : leukoplakia
조직병리학적 특징 : hyperkeratosis, acanthosis
출처: 250926_oral potentially malignant disorder_윤혜정 p.2, 
p.4-9
해설:
Leukoplakia 
- Most common OPMD
worldwide prevalence : 4.1%
- Malignant transformation rate : 4~22%
- Male predilection : 60~80% of cases
- Older than 40 years (average age = 6- ; 
similar to that of cancer)
조직병리학적 특징 : hyperkeratosis and acanthosis
- A thickened keratin layer : hyperparakeratosis 
or heperorhokeratosis
- A thickened spinous layer
조직학적 특징은 수업시간에 강조하신 부분입니다 !
6. 임상적으로 하얗게 보이는 것은 epithelial 
hyperplasia(hyperkeratosis or acanthosis)를
나타낸다.(24,21,20,19)
History of trauma → ( 1 )
잡아당겼을 때 사라짐 → ( 2 )
Candidal infection → ( 3 )
Band like T cell infiltration → ( 4 )
Perinuclear condensation → ( 5 )
답: 
1-frictional keratosis
2-leukoedema
3-Hyperplastic candidiasis
4-Lichen planus
5-White sponge nevus
참고: 250926 _윤혜정_Oral Potentially malignant 
lesion, p.30
해설:
강의 내용이 변경됐지만 그대로 출제하신 것 같습니다.
*History of trauma/irritation : Frictional keratosis
*Disappears when stretched : Leukoedema
*Candida infection: Hyperplastic candidiasis
*Reticulation, Band like T cell infiltration 
: Lichen planus
*Velvety, fissured surface, perinuclear condensation: 
white sponge nevus

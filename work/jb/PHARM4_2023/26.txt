1. CYP2D6 유전형을 분석한 결과 poor 
metabolizer가 이 약물을 적용하면 치료효과가
감소한다.
1) Warfarin
2) Lidocaine
3) Tramadol
4) Diazepam
5) Carbamazepine
답: 3) tramadol
해설:
- CYP2D6 poor metabolizer: avoid codeine use 
because of possibility of diminished analgesia.
- The CYP2D6 enzyme converts tramadol to the 
active metabolite, O-desmethyltramadol, which is 
up to 6 times more potent than tramadol in 
producing analgesia.
참고: 211202_ 백정화_약물상호작용, p.22-23
2. carbamazepine - induced SJS 유전자?
답: HLA-B 15:02 allele
해설:
The HLA-B*15:02 allele is strongly associated with 
carbamazepine-induced SJS/TEN in populations 
where this allele is most common, such as in 
Southeast Asia.
참고: 221201_백정화_약물상호작용, p.49
3. Warfarin 복용 시 피부 괴사가 일어나는 경우
어떤 gene의 문제인가?
답: protein C (PROC) or protein S (PROS1)
해설:
Aside from bleeding, skin necrosis is an 
infrequent, but major, complication of warfarin in 
patients who have a thrombophilic history or after 
administration of large loading doses of warfarin 
particularly without simultaneous initial use of 
heparin.
Hereditary or acquired deficiencies of protein C 
(PROC) or protein S (PROS1) have been associated 
with tissue necrosis following warfarin 
administration.
참고: 221201_백정화_약물상호작용, p.46

241107 _이윤실 _항불안제_p.19
• Inhibitors of CYP3A4, such as diltiazem, 
cimetidine, erythromycin, fluconazole, itraconazole, 
ketoconazole, saquinavir, and verapamil, were 
shown to significantly increase the Conc. of orally 
administered midazolam.
• Inhibitors of CYP2C19, such as cimetidine, 
imipramine, fluoxetine, chloramphenicol, and 
omeprazole, may lead to increased and prolonged 
sedation.
• Cytochrome P450 inducers, such as rifampin, 
carbamazepine, and phenytoin, can cause a 
markedly decreased Conc. of oral midazolam.
+구글링 결과 1번 문제의 phenobarbital 도
CYP450 유도제입니다.

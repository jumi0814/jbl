2. Iodine의 특징으로 맞는 것은? (보기미복원)
1) have relatively high toxicity
2) most valuable, powerful antiseptic agent
3) cannot kill virus
4) have no disclosing activity
5) not soluble in alcohol
답:2)
해설:
1번 보기만 복원되어 2-5번 보기는 ppt를
바탕으로 임의로 복원하였습니다.
다음은 ppt에 언급된 Iodine의 일반적인
특징입니다.
• Most valuable, powerful antiseptic agent and 
have bactericidal, sporicidal, fungicidal and 
virucidal effects
•Have relative low toxicity
•Have rapid action, high penetrating ability and 
active over a wide range of pH
•Slightly soluble in the water, moderately soluble 
in alcohol and glycerin
•Not affected by presence of organic matters and 
incompatible with alkali
•Have disclosing activity

3. bis-biguanide의 작용으로 옳지 않은 것은?
1) Activity is reduced by organic matters
답:1)
해설:
다음은 Bis-biguanides의 general features입니다.
•Bactericidal against G(+) and G(-) bacteria
•Ineffective against tubercle bacilli, spore and 
viruses
•Have cationic detergent action
•Activity is not reduced by organic matters
•Currently valuable agent for the control of 
dental plaque
•Sensitive to light

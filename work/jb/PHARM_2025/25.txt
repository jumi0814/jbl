Hypochlorite substances
* General features
• Potent germicidal agents
• Highly reactive with organic matters
• Fairly stable in alkali
• Antibacterial effect is increased with high 
temperature
• Have solvent action at high concentration
* Clinical uses
• Root canal irrigation and debridement
• Tx. Of periapical abscess, fistula and wet 
dressing of infected wounds
* Precautions
• Deteriorate on standing and by the light
• Organic matters decrease antibacterial effect
